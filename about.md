## Who we are

**Meow Apps Studio** is an independent mobile application studio. We are committed to creating
aesthetically pleasing and powerful apps for both iOS and Android platforms. We have already
launched a number of outstanding applications such as Meow Money Manager, Meow Todo List & Task,
Meow Habits and Meow Mood Diary — and we truly appreciate your attention. Your support is our
greatest motivation to keep moving forward.

## What we build

- **Cute, focused experiences** — a cat-themed visual language that makes everyday tools enjoyable.
- **Practical features** — bookkeeping, budgeting, habit tracking, planning and journaling that actually help.
- **Privacy first** — data stays on your device by default; no account is required to get started.
- **Cross platform** — the same experience on Google Play and the App Store.
