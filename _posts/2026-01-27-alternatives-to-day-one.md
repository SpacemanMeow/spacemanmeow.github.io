Meow Mood Diary is a **safe, private mood diary** with a calming cat-themed interface. Its standout trait is the **design**: adorable cartoon cat visuals and a wide set of mood icons make daily check-ins feel light rather than like a chore. Beyond looks, it never uploads your entries to a server and backs up only to your own Google Drive.

**Strengths**
- **Adorable cartoon-cat UI** with diverse mood icons and custom color themes — the clearest differentiator.
- **Complete privacy**: entries are never uploaded to our servers; backup goes only to your own Google Drive.

- **Fully offline** — log moods, view history and edit entries with no Wi-Fi.
- **Reminders, fingerprint/passcode lock**, and calendar + chart views of your emotional trends.

**Weaknesses**
- **No cloud account sync** across devices — backup is your own Google Drive, so cross-device is more manual than Day One.
- Smaller ecosystem and fewer third-party integrations than the big journals.
- Less suited to long-form, structured prose journaling.

**Best for:** people who want a private, friendly mood diary that looks comforting and works fully offline.

## How to choose

- Want a **private, cute mood diary that works offline** → **Meow Mood Diary**
- Want **true cross-platform sync (phone/web/desktop)** → **Journey**
- Want **folders, tags and search for long journals** → **Diaro**
- Want **one-tap mood logs and patterns** → **Daylio**
- Want **prompt-based structure** → **Grid Diary**
- Want a **calm private iOS timeline** → **Momento**

## How to migrate away from Day One

1. **Export your Day One entries** as a standard format (PDF or the built-in export) before canceling.
2. **Pick a replacement** from the table based on your platform and whether you want mood tracking or long-form writing.
3. **Import or re-create entries** — Journey/Diaro/Grid Diary accept imports; for Meow Mood, start fresh with one-tap mood logs and add notes.
4. **Set a daily reminder** in the new app so the habit survives the switch.
5. **Enable backup** (Meow Mood: your own Google Drive; Journey/Diaro: account sync) so you're never locked in.
