A **floating assistant** puts a small button on the screen edge that triggers the actions you reach for constantly — back, home, recent, lock, screenshot — without hunting for a physical key. **Floating Assistant** is an **edge floating-ball app for Android** with deep customization. Below we compare it with **Assistive Touch**, the popular floating-button class, for everyday multitasking.

## What a floating assistant needs

- **Edge floating ball** — a thumb-reachable button that stays out of the way.
- **Quick actions** — back, home, recent, lock screen, screenshot, split-screen.
- **Custom buttons and gestures** — map what you actually use.
- **Lightweight** — it shouldn't lag the phone.
- **Local and private** — no account, no upload.

Floating Assistant is an **edge floating-ball app** for Android with a rich action set and customization.

**Strengths for everyday use**
- **Edge ball** — a thumb-reachable button on any screen.
- **Deep actions** — back, home, recent, lock, screenshot, split-screen, volume, brightness.
- **Custom buttons and gestures** — map taps, swipes, and long-presses to what you use.
- **Lightweight and local** — runs on-device, no account.
- **Multi-window friendly** — quick split-screen for true multitasking.

**Weaknesses for everyday use**
- **VIP for more** — extra actions and gestures sit behind the upgrade.
- **Overlay permission** — you grant access once.

**Best for:** Android users who want a flexible, customizable floating control.

- [Google Play — Floating Assistant](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.glgjing.floating.apps.assistive.touch.pro)

Assistive Touch (package `mtr.assistivetouch.easytools`) is a popular floating-button app offering virtual keys and shortcuts.

**Strengths for everyday use**
- **Floating ball + virtual keys** — back, home, recent, lock, and more as on-screen buttons.
- **Quick app shortcuts** — launch favorites from the ball.
- **Brightness/volume controls** — handy without physical keys.
- **Free to start** — basic use with IAP for extras.

**Weaknesses for everyday use**
- **Standard action set** — less deep than Floating Assistant's gestures.
- **Ads / IAP** — promotional content and packs vary.
- **Lighter customization** — fewer ways to remap complex flows.

**Best for:** users who want simple virtual keys to replace physical buttons.

- [Google Play — Assistive Touch](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=mtr.assistivetouch.easytools)

## Which should you pick?

- Want **deep actions + gestures + multitasking** → **Floating Assistant**.
- Want **simple virtual keys, free** → **Assistive Touch**.
