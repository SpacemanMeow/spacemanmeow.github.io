A **mood diary** helps you notice patterns in how you feel — not by writing essays, but by logging a feeling in ten seconds and letting the trends speak. **Meow Mood** is a **private mood tracker** for Android and iOS that makes logging gentle: tap an emotion, add context, and watch the charts. Below we compare the apps people actually install — **Meow Mood, Day One, Journey, Daylio, and Grid Diary** — across mood tracking, privacy, price, and platforms.

## What a mood diary should do

- **Fast logging** — pick an emotion in one tap, not a blank page.
- **Context tags** — note sleep, people, or weather without a paragraph.
- **Trends** — a calendar and charts that show when lows cluster.
- **Privacy** — offline-first, with data that stays yours.
- **Export** — PDF or text to reflect or share with a professional.
- **A nudge** — a daily reminder that builds the habit.

## Meow Mood, in depth

Meow Mood is a **cat-themed mood and diary app** for Android and iOS. Logging is one tap: choose an emotion, add optional tags or a short note, done.

**Strengths for mood tracking**
- **One-tap logging** — an emotion icon beats a blank text field on a bad day.
- **Fully offline** — it works with no signal and stores entries on your device.
- **No upload by default** — data stays local; backup goes only to your own Google Drive if you enable it.
- **Trend views** — calendars and charts surface when low moods cluster.
- **Lock** — a fingerprint or passcode keeps entries yours.
- **PDF export** — reflect or share with a professional.
- **Daily reminder** — the nudge that makes it a habit.

**Weaknesses for mood tracking**
- **Lighter rich media** — fewer photos/audio bells than Day One.
- **Advanced export (CSV) is VIP** — free users get PDF.

**Best for:** people who want a **free mood diary** that's quick, cute, and private.

- [App Store — Meow Mood Diary](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6767560237)


## Day One, in depth

Day One is the original, most-awarded digital journal — praised by *The New York Times* and *Wired* as a "completely private digital space." It syncs across Android, iOS, Mac, and web.

**Strengths for mood tracking**
- **Rich entries** — text, photos, video, audio, location, and weather in one note.
- **Templates and prompts** — structured starts when you're stuck.
- **Encrypted sync** — entries follow you across every device.
- **Mature ecosystem** — a decade of updates, the most polished journaling app.

**Weaknesses for mood tracking**
- **Free tier is limited** — caps on devices and photos; full use needs Silver or Gold.
- **Cloud sync by design** — entries live on Day One's servers; privacy depends on trusting the vendor.
- **Heavier to start** — a blank rich entry is more friction than a one-tap mood.

**Best for:** people who want a full-feature journal and accept a subscription plus cloud sync.

- [Google Play — Day One](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.dayoneapp.dayone)
- [App Store — Day One](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1044867788)

## How the others compare

- **Journey** is a travel-leaning journal with Drive-backed sync; great for trips, less focused on quick mood logging.
- **Daylio** logs mood as icons plus a note, free with a subscription for history — the closest pure mood-tracking cousin to Meow Mood.
- **Grid Diary** structures entries through daily questions; good if prompts help you write.

## Which should you pick?

- Want **fast, private, one-tap mood logging** → **Meow Mood**.
- Want **a rich, synced life journal** and accept a subscription → **Day One**.
- Want **icon-only micro logs** → **Daylio**.
- Want **prompted structure** → **Grid Diary**.
