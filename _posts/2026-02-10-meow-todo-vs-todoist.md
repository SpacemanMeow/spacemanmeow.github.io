Choosing a **task manager** often comes down to complexity vs simplicity. This **Meow Todo vs Todoist** comparison helps you pick the **to-do list app** that matches how you actually work, with 2026 pricing and real feature limits.

![Meow Todo vs Todoist](/assets/images/apps/meow-todo-list/screen-compare.webp)

## At a glance

| | Meow Todo | Todoist |
|---|---|---|
| **Price** | Free core; optional VIP | Free (Beginner) + Pro ~$60/yr ($7/mo) |
| **Free tier limits** | Generous core | ~5 active projects, limited reminders |
| **Subtasks** | Yes | Yes |
| **Calendar** | Day / week / month view | Calendar integration (Pro) |
| **Natural-language dates** | Manual picking | Yes ("every mon 9am") |
| **Recurring** | Daily/weekly/monthly/end-of-month | Yes |
| **Sync** | Google Drive / WebDav (your account) | Proprietary cloud |
| **Collaboration** | Personal lists | Shared projects (Pro) |
| **CSV export** | Yes | No |
| **Platforms** | Android, iOS | Android, iOS, web, desktop |
| **Style** | Cute cat theme | Minimal productivity |
| **Privacy** | Local-first + fingerprint lock | Cloud account |

## Meow Todo, in detail

Meow Todo is a **cute task manager** that stores everything on your device. Core features are free, and your data stays private behind an optional fingerprint lock, with backup to your own Drive or WebDav.

- **Lists and tags** to organize work, study, and home.
- **Subtasks** for deeper breakdown.
- **Priority** levels (high, medium, low, normal).
- **Recurring tasks** — daily, weekly, monthly, yearly, end-of-month.
- **Calendar view** (day/week/month) and completion stats.
- **Daily and due-task notifications**.
- **CSV export** — export your tasks for backup or migration.
- **400+ cute icons** and dark mode.

## Todoist, in detail

Todoist is the productivity powerhouse. Its strength is speed and ecosystem: type "Submit tax every Apr 15" and it parses the date; connect it to hundreds of integrations; earn Karma for completed tasks.

- **Natural-language input** for dates and recurrence.
- **Projects, labels, filters, and views** for heavy organizing.
- **Integrations** (calendar, Slack, Zapier, etc.).
- **Shared projects** for teams (Pro/Business).
- **Apps everywhere** — web, desktop, mobile.

## When Meow Todo wins

- You want a **free task manager** without a paywall on core features.
- You prefer **local-first privacy** with your own Drive/WebDav backup.
- You like a **cute, calming interface** over a corporate one.
- You need **CSV export** of your tasks.

## When Todoist wins

- You want **natural-language** date entry ("every monday at 9").
- You need **team collaboration** and shared projects.
- You live inside a **web and desktop** workflow with integrations.

## Verdict by user type

- **Student / personal planner** → Meow Todo (free, private, CSV).
- **Solo power user** → either; Meow for privacy, Todoist for integrations.
- **Team / shared lists** → Todoist (shared projects need Pro).
- **Minimalist** → Microsoft To Do or Google Tasks (both free).

## Bottom line

Pick **Todoist** if collaboration and natural-language capture are essential. Pick **Meow Todo** if you want a **private, cute, free-core to-do list app** with subtasks, calendar, and CSV export. Both are strong **task managers** — the right one fits your workflow, not the other way around.
