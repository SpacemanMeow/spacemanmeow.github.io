Between lectures, assignments, and part-time shifts, students need a **task manager** that is fast and free. **Meow Todo** is a **free to-do list app for students** with subtasks, a calendar view, and reminders that survive a busy semester. Here's how it compares with other student-friendly options, plus a four-step setup.

## Why students skip heavy task apps

- **Syllabus deadlines** hide across many courses.
- **Group projects** need clear subtasks.
- A **paywall on reminders** is the last thing you need on a student budget.

## What a student task manager needs

- **Free core** features.
- **Subtasks** for multi-step assignments.
- **Calendar** to see the whole week at a glance.
- **Reminders** for due dates.
- **Cloud sync** across phone and laptop.

| App | Price | Student fit |

| **Microsoft To Do** | Free | Best with Outlook/Office |

Meow Todo delivers the core set above with no account required and a cute interface that makes daily logging painless.

## 4-step semester setup

1. Make a **list per course**.
2. Add assignments as tasks with **due dates and priorities**.
3. Break big ones into **subtasks** (research, draft, submit).
4. Check the **calendar** every morning.

## Tips that actually stick

- **One list per course** keeps syllabi from blending together.
- **Recurring tasks** for weekly labs or shifts — set them once, done for the term.
- **Daily reminder** surfaces today's due items each morning.
- **CSV export** at finals to hand in or archive your planner.
