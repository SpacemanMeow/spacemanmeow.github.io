WidgetSmith is a favorite on iOS, but Android users searching for **WidgetSmith alternatives** need a different tool, and even iPhone users may want more. Here are the best **widget apps** across platforms.

![WidgetSmith alternatives overview](/assets/images/apps/one-widget/screen-alt.webp)

## Why look beyond WidgetSmith

- **No Android version** — iPhone-only.
- You want **more styles** or live system data (battery, RAM).
- You prefer **ready widgets** over designing each one.

## The best WidgetSmith alternatives

| App | Price (2026) | Platforms | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **One Widget** | Free One UI + unlock | Android | 500+ ready, themed widgets |
| **KWGT** | Free + Pro key (~$4.99) | Android | Custom widget editor |
| **Widgy** | Free + IAP (~$4.99) | iOS | Highly custom iOS widgets |
| **iOS native** | Free (built-in) | iOS 16+ | Apple app widgets |

### 1. One Widget — best for Android

A **Material You widgets** and beyond app with 500+ ready widgets in One UI, Nothing, Glass, and Material You styles — clock, battery, RAM, toggles, shortcuts, and tools.

### 2. KWGT — best for custom design

A powerful widget editor if you want to build your own (see [One Widget vs KWGT](/posts/one-widget-vs-kwgt/)).

### 3. Widgy — best for custom iOS widgets

A flexible iOS widget builder with templates and deep customization; free with in-app purchases.

### 4. iOS native — best built-in

On iPhone, iOS 16+ supports customizable lock and home screen widgets without a third party for Apple apps.

## Which WidgetSmith alternative fits you?

- On **Android** and want ready widgets → **One Widget**
- Want full control → KWGT (Android) or Widgy (iOS)
- Want cute themes → WidgetClub
- Just need Apple widgets → iOS native

## Pick by device

- **Android phone (Samsung/Pixel/Nothing)** → One Widget (free One UI set) or KWGT.
- **iPhone, bespoke design** → Widgy.
- **iPhone, minimal** → iOS native + WidgetSmith.
