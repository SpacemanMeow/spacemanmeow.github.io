## What the best flip clock should do

A great **fullscreen clock** should:
- Show a satisfying **flip animation**
- Offer themes and 12/24-hour modes
- Include **widgets**
- Double as a **nightstand** or focus clock

## Only Clock in depth

From the app itself:

- **3D flip clock** with 24-hour support and seconds.
- Day of week and date display.
- **Floating clock** (always-on overlay) with permission.
- Themes — black, white, cat, sky, purple, earth, space, panda, planet, and more.
- **White noise** background to aid focus.
- Multiple **clock widgets** (flat and split styles).
- Works as a **desk or nightstand clock**.

![Only Clock themes](/assets/images/apps/only-clock/screen-themes.webp)

**Where it wins**
- Cross-platform (Android + iOS), including a spare-device clock.
- Themes, white noise, and a floating clock in one app.
- Both fullscreen and widget modes.

**Where it's lighter**
- The retro flip is stylized rather than a photoreal mechanical replica (use Retroflip if you want a stricter retro look).

## How the others compare

- **Retroflip** is a lightweight, free fullscreen retro flip clock — great for the pure look, usually with ads/IAP and no noise.
- **Flip Clock Screen Saver** bundles screensaver, digital clock, stopwatch, and a Pomodoro timer.
- **Retro Desk Clock** leans into the vintage desk aesthetic.
