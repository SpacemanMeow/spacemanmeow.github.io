A good **Android widget** puts the info you check most — time, weather, battery, today's calendar — on the home screen without opening an app. **One Widget** is a **cat-themed widget pack** for Android that ships ready-made widgets you place in seconds. Below we compare it with **KWGT**, the deep-customization toolkit, across setup, styling, and price.

## What an Android widget app should do

- **Ready-made types** — clock, weather, calendar, battery, steps, countdown.
- **No design degree** — tap, place, done.
- **Custom color, size, and transparency** — match the wallpaper.
- **Lightweight and local** — no heavy cloud or account.
- **Cute without trying** — a friendly look that doesn't feel corporate.
- **Fair price** — core widgets shouldn't cost much.

## One Widget, in depth

One Widget is a **cat-themed widget pack** for Android. It ships widgets for clock, weather, calendar, battery, steps, and countdown, ready to drop on the home screen.

**Strengths for Android users**
- **Ready-made widgets** — no editor, no code; place and resize.
- **Cute cat styling** — a friendly look across every widget.
- **Custom color, size, transparency** — tune to your wallpaper.
- **Local and light** — runs on-device, no account.
- **Multiple types** — clock, weather, calendar, battery, steps, countdown in one pack.

**Weaknesses for Android users**
- **Less depth than KWGT** — you style, but you don't build from scratch.
- **VIP for more** — extra widgets and deeper styling sit behind the upgrade.

**Best for:** people who want **Android widgets** that look good immediately.

- [Google Play — One Widget](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.glgjing.galaxy.nothing.glass.material.widget)


## KWGT, in depth

KWGT (Kustom Widget Maker, package `org.kustom.widget`) is the most powerful widget creator on Android — you design every pixel with its editor and a formula language ("Kode").

**Strengths for Android users**
- **Unlimited design** — clocks, weather, music, system stats, animations.
- **Kode + komponents** — reusable parts and logic for true customization.
- **One-time Pro Key** — unlock is a purchase, not a subscription.

**Weaknesses for Android users**
- **Steep learning curve** — building a widget from zero takes real effort.
- **Launcher-dependent** — best with a third-party launcher that supports KWGT.
- **Not ready-made** — there is no "just place it" cat clock; you make it.

**Best for:** Android tinkerers who want total control.

- [Google Play — KWGT](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=org.kustom.widget)

## Which should you pick?

- Want **cute widgets now, no editor** → **One Widget**.
- Want **total control and don't mind learning** → **KWGT**.
