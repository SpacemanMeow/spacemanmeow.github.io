Meow Mood is a **safe, private mood diary** with a calming cat-themed interface. Its standout trait is the **design**: adorable cartoon-cat visuals and a wide set of mood icons make daily check-ins feel light rather than clinical. Beyond looks, it never uploads entries to a server and backs up only to your own Google Drive.

### Strengths
- **Adorable cartoon-cat UI** with diverse mood icons and custom color themes — the clearest differentiator.
- **Complete privacy**: entries are never uploaded to our servers; backup goes only to your own Google Drive.
- **One-tap quick mood logging** — record how you feel without writing long entries.
- **Emotion and context tags** to understand *why* you felt a certain way.
- **Fully offline** — log moods, view history and edit entries with no Wi-Fi.
- **Reminders, fingerprint/passcode lock**, and calendar + chart views of emotional trends.
- **Detailed diary entries** and customizable daily reminders; PDF export.

### Weaknesses
- **No rich media** — it's mood + notes, not a photo/audio scrapbook.
- **No cloud account sync** across devices — backup is your own Google Drive, so cross-device is more manual than Day One.
- Smaller ecosystem and fewer third-party integrations.
- Less suited to long-form, structured prose journaling.
- Some advanced stats sit behind VIP.

### Best for
People who want a private, friendly mood diary that looks comforting and works fully offline.

### Strengths
- **Rich media** — photos, audio and video attached to entries, plus location and weather.
- **Cloud sync (E2E encrypted)** across Android, iOS, Mac and web — seamless cross-device.
- **Polished writing experience** with prompts, templates and a powerful search.

- **Paid subscription** (~$35/year) for the features most people use.

- iPhone-leaning heritage; the Android/web experience lags iOS.

## How they compare where it matters

### Privacy model
Meow Mood is offline-first: entries never leave your device unless you back up to your own Google Drive. Day One syncs to its encrypted cloud. Both protect data, but Meow Mood is the stronger "nothing leaves my phone" choice.

### Mood vs memory
Meow Mood is purpose-built for *emotion*: mood icons, context tags, trends and reminders that surface patterns. Day One is purpose-built for *memory*: rich entries with media and a timeline. If you want to understand your feelings, Meow Mood; if you want to document your life, Day One.

### Media and richness
Day One clearly wins on media — photos, audio, video, location. Meow Mood is deliberately lean (mood + notes). That leanness is also its speed: a check-in takes seconds.

### Platforms and sync
Day One covers Android, iOS, Mac and web with seamless sync. Meow Mood covers Android and iOS with self-controlled backup. Heavy Mac/web users will feel Day One's breadth.

### Price
Meow Mood is free at the core; Day One is a ~$35/year subscription. For pure mood tracking, Meow Mood is the lower-cost path.

## Which should you choose

- **Choose Meow Mood** if your goal is tracking emotions privately and seeing patterns, you prefer offline-first privacy, and want a cute, quick daily check-in.
- **Choose Day One** if you want a full multimedia journal that syncs everywhere and keeps a written, photo-rich life record.
- **They're complementary**: many users keep Meow Mood for daily mood check-ins and Day One for longer journaling — but if you only want one, let the mood-vs-memory question decide.

## Other mood apps worth considering

A two-app comparison can't cover every reason people switch journals, and the app you are leaving changes what you should look for:

- A **Daylio alternative** is the most common search here: the priority is one-tap mood logging with activity tags and a calendar, without writing paragraphs. Meow Mood covers that loop and keeps it on-device.
- A **MoodKit alternative** usually means you want structured mood notes and thought records rather than a simple five-level scale; that is a different kind of journal, and neither app compared here is built for it.
- A **Bearable alternative** fits people correlating mood with sleep or other daily factors — more structured tracking than a daily capsule.
- A **Journey alternative** or **Reflectly alternative** is the right search when the missing piece is guided prompts and a polished writing experience; Day One is the closer match of the two.

## Typical scenarios

- **Daily mood check-in** — Meow Mood's one-tap mood logging fits a 20-second evening habit; Day One wants a fuller entry, better for weekend reflections.
- **Strict privacy** — Meow Mood keeps entries on-device with no account; Day One's encrypted cloud is excellent but still server-stored.
- **Rich memory-keeping** — Day One shines with photos, audio, and cross-device sync; Meow Mood deliberately stays lean.
- **Use both** — many log moods in Meow Mood and write long entries in Day One; if you only want one, let mood-vs-memory decide.
