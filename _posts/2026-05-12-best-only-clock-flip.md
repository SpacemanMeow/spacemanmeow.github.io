A **flip clock app** turns any phone or tablet into a calming, retro timepiece. **Only Clock** is a **fullscreen flip clock** with smooth flip animation, themes, a floating mode, white noise, and a focus timer. Below we compare it with the popular free **Flip Clock** and what each is actually good at.

## What a flip clock app should do

- **Smooth flip animation** — the card flip should feel physical, not janky.
- **Fullscreen, readable** — big digits you can glance at across a room.
- **Themes** — retro, cat, minimal, or photo-based to match a room.
- **Widgets** — a clock on the home screen, not just inside the app.
- **White noise** — a bedside or focus sound without a second app.
- **Floating mode** — a clock over a video or while you work.

Only Clock is a **fullscreen flip-clock** for Android and iOS with a 3D flip animation and a broad theme library.

**Strengths**
- **Rich themes** — retro, cute cat scenes, minimal, and photo backgrounds; the clock matches your mood or room.
- **Smooth animation** — the flip transition is fluid on modern devices.
- **White noise** — built-in ambient sound for sleep and focus.
- **Floating overlay** — keep a clock above video or other apps.
- **Widgets** — flat and split styles with optional date.
- **Cross-platform** — Android and iOS, including a spare phone as a clock display.

**Weaknesses**
- **VIP for the best themes/sounds** — the top themes and noise are behind an unlock.
- **Heavier than minimal apps** — the animation and themes cost a little battery vs a static clock.

**Best for:** people who want a flip clock with personality, sound, and a floating mode.

Flip Clock (package `com.wssc.simpleclock`) is a popular **free flip-clock app** — a clean fullscreen flip animation, brightness and always-on controls, and the date, with no ads.

**Strengths**
- **Free and clean** — a no-nonsense retro flip look, no price, no ads.
- **Simple controls** — brightness and always-on are easy to reach.
- **Lightweight** — minimal battery and storage.

**Weaknesses**
- **Limited themes** — stays in the classic lane with little variation.
- **No white noise** — it's a silent clock.
- **No floating mode or rich widgets** — the time lives inside the app only.

**Best for:** anyone who wants a free, simple retro flip clock and nothing more.

- [Google Play — Flip Clock](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.wssc.simpleclock)
