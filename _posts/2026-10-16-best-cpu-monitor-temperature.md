When a phone gets hot it slows down — thermal throttling kicks in and frames drop. A **CPU temperature monitor** lets you see the heat before it costs performance. **CPU Monitor** is a **CPU temp app for Android** with real-time temperature, per-core load, and overheat alerts. Below we compare it with **CPU-Z**, the classic hardware-info tool, for spotting heat early.

## What a CPU temp monitor needs

- **Real-time temperature** — the current CPU temp at a glance.
- **Per-core frequency and load** — see which core is working hardest.
- **Overheat alerts** — a warning before throttling bites.
- **Light background use** — monitoring shouldn't itself heat the phone.
- **History** — a curve that shows when it spiked.

## CPU Monitor, in depth

CPU Monitor is a **CPU temperature and load monitor** for Android. The core — live temperature, per-core frequency, and load — is free; VIP adds deeper history and options.

**Strengths for heat watching**
- **Live CPU temperature** — a clear number you can watch while gaming or charging.
- **Per-core frequency and load** — spot the core pinned at 100%.
- **Overheat alerts** — get warned before throttling drops performance.
- **Lightweight** — minimal background cost, so monitoring doesn't add heat.
- **History curve** — see when temperature spiked during a session.

**Weaknesses for heat watching**
- **VIP for deeper history** — long-term logs sit behind the upgrade.
- **Sensor accuracy varies** — readings depend on what the device exposes.

**Best for:** people who want to catch overheating early, not just read specs.

- [Google Play — CPU Monitor](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.glgjing.stark)


## CPU-Z, in depth

CPU-Z (package `com.cpuid.cpu_z`) is the Android port of the well-known CPUID tool, focused on reporting hardware details.

**Strengths for heat watching**
- **Detailed hardware readout** — SoC name, architecture, per-core frequency, system, battery, and sensors.
- **Free** — no cost, no subscription.
- **Trusted source** — CPUID's reputation for accurate specs.

**Weaknesses for heat watching**
- **Info only** — it shows temperature but offers no alerts or advice.
- **No trend curve** — a snapshot, not a history.
- **Limited Android sensors** — the mobile build exposes less than the PC version.

**Best for:** users who want to read exact hardware specs and current temps.

- [Google Play — CPU-Z](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.cpuid.cpu_z)

## How the others compare

- **DevCheck** shows rich device info with a live view; free with IAP, but no overheat alerts.
- **AIDA64** is the deepest spec reader; great for details, not for heat warnings.

## Which should you pick?

- Want **real-time temp + per-core load + overheat alerts** → **CPU Monitor**.
- Want **free, detailed hardware specs** → **CPU-Z**.
