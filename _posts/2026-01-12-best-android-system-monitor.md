Most "cleaner" apps promise magic. **System Monitor** takes a clearer path: it **monitors** your phone and helps you **boost performance** without pretending to delete files it should not. It is an all-in-one **system monitor** for Android — and a smarter first step than a blind cleaner.

![System Monitor dashboard](/assets/images/apps/system-monitor/screen-dashboard.webp)

## What an all-in-one monitor should do

A good **android system monitor** should:
- Show **CPU temperature and frequency**
- Analyze **RAM** and offer a boost
- Report **battery health**
- Give a **storage overview**
- Warn on **overheating**

## The contenders at a glance

| App | Focus | Live monitoring | Boost | Battery | Price |

## System Monitor in depth

From the app itself:

- **CPU monitoring** — temperature, frequency, activity, usage curve, per-core, temperature ranking.
- **RAM analysis and one-tap boost** (with an ignore list).
- **Battery doctor** — temperature, health, voltage, level curve, saver modes (Long Life / Custom).
- **Storage overview** that highlights what is using space.
- **Overheat alarm and protection**.
- **Floating window** (status bar and desktop).
- RAM, CPU, and battery widgets.
- Multiple themes.

![System Monitor battery](/assets/images/apps/system-monitor/screen-battery.webp)

**Where it wins**
- Live, always-on monitoring with a floating window and widgets.
- Battery doctor with saver modes — rare in spec-only apps.
- One-tap boost plus an ignore list so you don't kill apps you want.

**Where it's lighter**
- It does **not** clean cache/junk (by design). For safe cleanup, pair it with Files by Google (see [CCleaner alternatives](/posts/alternatives-to-ccleaner/)).

## How the others compare

- **CPU-Z** is the quick spec sheet — SoC, battery, sensors — but no live floating window or alarms.
- **DevCheck** adds a clean sensor dashboard and battery info.
- **3C Toolbox** is a broad toolkit (monitoring, battery, tweaks) with many paid features.
- **AIDA64** is the deep reporting tool for spec lovers.

## How to start in 3 steps

1. Open System Monitor for the live dashboard.
2. Enable the **floating window** for always-on stats.
3. Turn on the **overheat alarm** and use one-tap boost when slow.

## Which should you pick?

- Want **live monitoring + boost + battery doctor** → **System Monitor**.
- Want a **spec sheet** → CPU-Z or AIDA64.
- Want a **sensor dashboard** → DevCheck.
- Want a **big toolkit** → 3C Toolbox.
