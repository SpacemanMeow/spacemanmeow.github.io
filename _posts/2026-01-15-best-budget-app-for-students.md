## Why students skip the spreadsheets

- **Ramen weeks and payday weeks** look very different — a monthly view hides the spikes.
- **Subscriptions** (Netflix, Spotify, iCloud) silently drain the balance.
- **Split costs with roommates** get messy without a record.
- **Reimbursements** (clubs, field trips) need tracking so you actually collect.

A **student budget app** turns that chaos into a one-tap habit.

## What a student budget app should have

- **Free core** — no subscription on a student loan.
- **No account required** — less friction, more privacy.
- **Recurring transactions** for rent, meal plan, Netflix, Spotify.
- **CSV export** for reimbursement or tax forms.
- **Multi-currency** for a study-abroad semester.

## 5-minute student setup

1. Create a "College" ledger.
2. Add **recurring entries**: rent, meal plan, subscriptions.
3. Set a **weekly budget** so the fun money is visible.
4. Log a coffee or textbook in two taps.
5. Export **CSV** at semester end for reimbursements or tax forms.

## Tips that actually stick

- **Pay yourself first**: log the big fixed costs as recurring so they're never a surprise.
- **Use a "Fun" category**: a small weekly allowance prevents all-or-nothing blowouts.
- **Split with roommates**: keep a shared ledger for groceries/utilities and settle monthly.
- **Study abroad**: open a second ledger in the local currency; Meow's 50+ symbols handle the conversion mentally.
