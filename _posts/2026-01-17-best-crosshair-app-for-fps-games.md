Some shooters hide or lack a clear reticle. **Crosshair Aim** is a **crosshair app for FPS games** that overlays a customizable crosshair on top of Android shooters — no game modification required. Below we compare the apps people actually use — **Crosshair Aim, Crosshair Master, CROSSARA, and AimLayer** — across styles, customization, and safety.

![Crosshair styles](/assets/images/apps/cross-hair/screen-styles.webp)

## What the best crosshair overlay should do

A good **custom crosshair** tool should:
- Offer many **styles**
- Allow **size, color, and position** control
- Work as a system **overlay**
- Stay invisible to the game engine

## The contenders at a glance

| App | Styles | Customization | In-game adjuster | Price |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Crosshair Aim** | **15+** (basic + advanced) | Size, color, scale, position, opacity | **Yes (floating)** | Free + unlock |
| **Crosshair Master** | Many presets | Style-dependent | Limited | Free (ads/IAP) |
| **CROSSARA** | Basic/Pro/Precision sets | Color, size, position | Yes | Free + IAP |
| **AimLayer** | Preset styles | Basic | Yes | Free |

## Crosshair Aim in depth

From the app itself:

- **15+ crosshair styles** (basic and advanced)
- Adjustable **size, color, scale, and position**
- **Transparency** control (default 85%)
- A floating adjuster to tweak while in-game
- Works with **almost all popular FPS and action shooting games**
- Generates a crosshair even for weapons without one
- Free basic styles; advanced via short unlock or purchase

![Crosshair in game](/assets/images/apps/cross-hair/screen-ingame.webp)

**Where it wins**
- Deep, transparent **customization** (size, color, scale, position).
- A **floating adjuster** so you never leave the game to fine-tune.
- No-root overlay — safe and game-independent.

**Where it's lighter**
- It's an overlay aid, not a cheat; it won't change hit registration.

## How the others compare

- **Crosshair Master** is a popular preset overlay with many styles and ads/IAP — quick to use, less granular.
- **CROSSARA** offers tiered design sets (Basic/Pro/Precision) with color and size control.
- **AimLayer** is a lightweight free overlay with simple presets.

## How to start in 3 steps

1. Grant the **overlay permission**.
2. Pick a style, color, and size.
3. Tap START to show the crosshair; drag to adjust in-game.

## Which should you pick?

- Want **full customization + in-game tweak** → **Crosshair Aim**.
- Want **quick presets** → Crosshair Master.
- Want **tiered design sets** → CROSSARA.
