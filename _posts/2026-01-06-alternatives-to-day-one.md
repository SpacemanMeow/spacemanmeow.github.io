Day One is a powerful journal, but its subscription and cloud model are not for everyone. These **alternatives to Day One** focus on privacy, mood, or simplicity.

![Day One alternatives overview](/assets/images/apps/meow-mood-diary/screen-alt.webp)

## Why people look beyond Day One

- **Paid annual subscription** (~$35/year in 2026, rebranded from Premium to "Silver").
- **Cloud sync** by default — some want local-only storage.
- **Prose-first** — less ideal for quick mood checks.

## The best Day One alternatives

| App | Price (2026) | Platforms | Style |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Meow Mood** | Free core + VIP | Android, iOS | Emotion-focused mood diary |
| **Journey** | Free + Cloud sub | Android, iOS, web, Win | Cross-platform journal |
| **Diarium** | One-time (~$20) | Android, iOS, Win, Mac | Local-first, many sources |
| **Grid Diary** | Free + Premium | Android, iOS | Prompt-based journal |
| **Daylio** | Free + Premium | Android, iOS | Micro mood logs |

### 1. Meow Mood — best for private mood tracking

A **mood diary** that never uploads your entries, with emotion and context tags, trends, PDF export, and offline use. Core logging is free.

### 2. Journey — best cross-platform journal

A popular **journaling app** with cloud sync, photos, and many platforms. Closest in scope to Day One for prose journaling.

### 3. Diarium — best local-first journal

A one-time-purchase journal that pulls from many sources (calendar, health, location) and keeps data local-first with optional sync.

### 4. Grid Diary — best prompt-based journal

Guided prompts help you write when you don't know where to start; free with a Premium tier.

### 5. Daylio — best mood-only tracker

Micro-journaling with moods and activities, great if you want speed over prose (free with extras).

## Which Day One alternative fits you?

- Want **privacy and mood focus** → **Meow Mood**
- Want a **Day One clone** → Journey
- Want **local-first + one purchase** → Diarium
- Want **AI/prompt guidance** → Grid Diary
- Want **quick mood logs** → Daylio

## How to migrate away from Day One

1. **Export** your Day One entries (multiple formats supported).
2. **Pick a replacement** using the table above based on privacy vs multimedia.
3. **Import or re-create** key entries — for mood tracking, start fresh logging daily.
4. **Set a daily reminder** to build the habit.
5. **Enable local backup** (Meow Mood: your own Google Drive) so you're never locked in.
