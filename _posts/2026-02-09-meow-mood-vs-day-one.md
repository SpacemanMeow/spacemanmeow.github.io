Both are journals, but they serve different needs. This **Meow Mood vs Day One** comparison helps you pick the right **journaling app** in 2026, with real pricing and privacy differences.

![Meow Mood vs Day One](/assets/images/apps/meow-mood-diary/screen-compare.webp)

## At a glance

| | Meow Mood | Day One |
|---|---|---|
| **Focus** | Mood and emotion tracking | Rich life journaling |
| **Price** | Free core + optional VIP | ~$35/yr (Silver) |
| **Privacy** | **Offline, never uploaded** | Cloud sync (E2E encrypted) |
| **Media** | Notes and tags | Photos, audio, video |
| **Export** | **PDF** | Multiple formats |
| **Reminders** | Daily mood reminders | Optional |
| **Platforms** | Android, iOS | Android, iOS, Mac, web |
| **Best for** | Emotion patterns, mental health | Multimedia memory-keeping |

## Meow Mood, in detail

Meow Mood is a **mood diary** that stores everything on your device. Core features are free, and your entries never leave the phone unless you back up to your own Google Drive.

- **5-level mood** scale plus detailed emotion tags.
- **Context tags** — work, family, health, money, weather, partner.
- Short text notes, timestamped.
- **Day / week / month** statistics and mood distribution.
- **PDF export** and daily reminders.
- **Fingerprint or passcode lock**.
- Fully **offline** and never uploaded.
- Cute cat theme and multiple color themes.

## Day One, in detail

Day One is the original premium digital journal. Its strength is capturing life in rich detail: long-form entries, photos, audio, video, and automatic metadata (location, weather, steps). It syncs via its own encrypted cloud.

- **Rich entries** with photos, audio, video.
- **Templates, prompts, and reminders**.
- **Cross-device sync** (phone, tablet, Mac, web).
- **Multiple export** formats for portability.
- A large, mature ecosystem.

## When Meow Mood wins

- You want a **private mood tracker** with zero uploads.
- You care about **emotion and context tags** more than media.
- You want **PDF export** of your diary.
- You prefer a **free core** experience.

## When Day One wins

- You want a full **life journal** with photos and audio.
- You rely on cross-device **cloud sync**.
- You are in the Apple ecosystem and want Mac/web access.

## Verdict by user type

- **Therapy / self-reflection** → Meow Mood (private, pattern-focused).
- **Memory-keeping / travel** → Day One (multimedia).
- **Apple-only writer** → Day One.
- **Privacy skeptic** → Meow Mood.

## Bottom line

Pick **Day One** for a multimedia life journal. Pick **Meow Mood** for a **private, emotion-focused mood diary** that respects your data and helps you spot emotional patterns. Both are excellent **journaling apps** — the right one fits what you want to capture.
