Mobile FPS games like PUBG Mobile and COD Mobile draw their own crosshair, and it isn't always where you want it. A **crosshair overlay** puts a reticle you control on top of the game — no root, just an overlay permission. **Crosshair Aim** is a **custom crosshair app** for Android with 80+ styles and an in-game floating adjuster. Below we compare it with **Crosshair Hero** for shooter use.

## What an FPS crosshair overlay needs

- **Overlay on top of the game** — no root, just a permission.
- **Many styles** — dot, cross, T, circle, and hybrids.
- **Color, size, transparency** — tune for your sight picture.
- **In-game adjuster** — move and resize without leaving the match.
- **Lightweight** — it must not cost frames.

## Crosshair Aim, in depth

Crosshair Aim is a **custom crosshair overlay** for Android with a large style library and a floating adjuster you drag over the game.

**Strengths for FPS players**
- **80+ styles** — basic and advanced reticles for any taste.
- **In-game floating adjuster** — resize and reposition live, without closing the game.
- **Color, size, transparency** — dial in contrast against any map.
- **No root** — works with the standard overlay permission.
- **Lightweight** — minimal impact on gameplay.

**Weaknesses for FPS players**
- **VIP for advanced styles** — the deepest library is behind the upgrade.
- **Overlay permission required** — you grant access once; some launchers need a toggle.

**Best for:** mobile shooter players who want a reticle tuned to them.

- [Google Play — Crosshair Aim](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.glgjing.crosshair.aim.fps.game)


## Crosshair Hero, in depth

Crosshair Hero (package `me.okitastudio.crosshairherofps`) is a popular **crosshair overlay app** with 30+ preset styles, custom PNG support, and color/size/position tweaks.

**Strengths for FPS players**
- **30+ presets** — recognizable crosshair styles you tap and go.
- **Custom PNG** — import your own image as the reticle.
- **Color, size, position** — quick adjustments in the app.
- **Free to start** — basic use with IAP for style packs.

**Weaknesses for FPS players**
- **Fewer styles than Crosshair Aim** — 30+ vs 80+.
- **App-side adjuster** — you tune before launching, not always live mid-match.
- **IAP / ad-supported** — style packs and ads vary.

**Best for:** players who want a recognizable preset crosshair in seconds.

- [Google Play — Crosshair Hero](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=me.okitastudio.crosshairherofps)

## Which should you pick?

- Want **80+ styles + a live in-game adjuster** → **Crosshair Aim**.
- Want a **quick preset crosshair** → **Crosshair Hero**.
