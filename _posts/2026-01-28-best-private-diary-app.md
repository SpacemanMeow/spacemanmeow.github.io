If privacy matters more than cloud features, you need a **private diary app** that never ships your thoughts to a server. **Meow Mood** is an **offline mood diary** built around that promise. Here's how it compares with other privacy-focused options, plus what to check before you commit.

![Private diary logging](/assets/images/apps/meow-mood-diary/screen-private.webp)

## What a private journal needs

- **No uploads** by default.
- **Lock** (fingerprint or passcode).
- Local **backup** you control.
- Easy **export**.

Meow Mood meets all four.

## Why Meow Mood stays private

- Entries are stored **only on your device**.
- Backup goes to **your own Google Drive**, not a vendor cloud.
- A **fingerprint or passcode lock** protects the app.
- **PDF export** lets you keep a copy anywhere.

## What to check before you trust an app

1. **Where is the data?** On-device > optional cloud > cloud-only.
2. **Who can read it?** A local lock plus optional encryption beats a server you don't control.
3. **Can you leave?** Export to PDF/text/JSON so you're never trapped.
4. **Account required?** Fewer accounts mean fewer breach surfaces. Meow Mood needs no account.

## Tips for a private habit

- **Set a daily reminder** so journaling becomes routine, not a chore.
- **Use context tags** (work, family, health) to find patterns later without re-reading everything.
- **Export monthly** to PDF as a personal backup.
