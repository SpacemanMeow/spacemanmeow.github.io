Building a habit is mostly about showing up daily, and the right app makes that satisfying instead of stressful. **Meow Habits** is a **cute habit tracker** for Android and iOS that turns routines into streaks, charts, and small daily wins. Below we compare the apps people actually install — **Meow Habits, Streaks, Habitica and Loop** — across build/break modes, platforms, privacy, and price.

## What a habit tracker should do

- **Build and break** — track "drink water" and "stop scrolling" in one place.
- **Flexible periods** — daily, weekly, monthly, or a custom cadence.
- **Visible progress** — bars, pies, and a calendar you actually glance at.
- **Cross-platform** — phone and tablet, and a second OS if your household mixes them.
- **Privacy** — offline-first with a lock, not a mandatory cloud.
- **Export** — CSV for review or sharing with a coach.

Meow Habits is a **cat-themed habit tracker** for Android and iOS. The core — tracking, streaks, and charts — is free; VIP adds CSV export and extra customization.

**Strengths**
- **Cross-platform** — the same app on Android and iOS, which is rare (Streaks is iOS-only, Loop is Android-only).
- **Build and break modes** — track positive routines and habits to drop.
- **Flexible periods** — daily, weekly, monthly, or custom, with bars, pies, and a calendar view.
- **Offline + lock** — works with no signal and secures data behind a fingerprint.
- **Backups** — Google Drive or WebDav, your choice.
- **Templates** — start a habit with one tap.

**Weaknesses**
- **CSV export is VIP** — free users can't export history.
- **Lighter coaching** — no RPG or behavioral-science nudges like Habitica or Fabulous.

**Best for:** people who want a **free habit tracker** that works on both major mobile OSes with privacy.

Streaks (by Crunchy Bagel) is an **Apple Design Award winner** and the cleanest habit to-do on iOS. It integrates with Apple Health to auto-complete goals like steps or heart rate.

**Strengths**
- **Dead-simple** — pick a task, check it daily, don't break the chain.
- **Health integration** — auto-tracks walking, water, and more via Apple Health.
- **Polished** — best-in-class iOS design and widgets.
- **One-time price** — no subscription.

**Weaknesses**
- **iOS only** — there is no Android app, so mixed-OS households can't share.
- **Paid upfront** — no free tier; you buy before trying.
- **Capped at 24 tasks** — plenty for most, tight for power users.
- **No Android sync** — data stays in the Apple ecosystem.

**Best for:** iPhone-only users who want the most polished, Health-connected tracker.

- [Apple App Store — Streaks](https://apps.apple.com/app/id963034692)

## How the others compare

- **Habitica** turns habits into an RPG: complete tasks, gain XP, fight monsters. Free with a subscription for cosmetic and convenience perks; great for motivation, heavier than a simple tracker.
- **Loop** is a free, open-source, Android-only tracker with no account and strong privacy — the minimalist's choice, but iOS users are left out.

## Which should you pick?

- Want **free + cute + both platforms + private** → **Meow Habits**.
- Want **the slickest iOS-only tracker with Health** → **Streaks**.
- Want **game-like motivation** → **Habitica**.
- Want **minimal open-source on Android** → **Loop**.
