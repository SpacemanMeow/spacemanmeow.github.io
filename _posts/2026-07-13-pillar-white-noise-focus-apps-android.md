## Why white noise app matters on Android

On Android, background playback is the make-or-break feature: sound must keep running with the screen off and sip battery while you sleep, because that is exactly when you need it. Meow FM adds 15+ ambient sounds, a sleep timer with 15/30/60-minute presets and a desktop clock for focus, and a home-screen clock widget turns the phone into a bedside display — something iPhone widgets do far less naturally.

## What to look for in a white noise app

Meow FM is a white noise app for Android and iOS with 15+ fully offline ambient sounds, a sleep timer, mixable layers and a desktop clock, free to start with a one-time unlock. A good sound app should need no account, keep audio running with the screen locked without draining battery, auto-stop with a sleep timer, offer a variety of rain/storm/forest/fireplace scenes you can stack, play offline without signal, and show a clock or focus timer for study sessions.

- **Privacy & account** — most sound apps don't need an account; you just press play. Prefer ones that work fully offline so nothing depends on a server you don't control.
- **Background playback & battery** — audio must continue with the screen locked and sip battery, not drain it overnight while you sleep.
- **Sleep timer** — auto turn-off so the noise stops after you doze off and doesn't run till morning.
- **Sound variety & mixing** — rain, storm, forest, fireplace; ideally layers you can stack (rain + thunder) into a custom scene.
- **Offline playback** — no signal, no problem on a plane, subway or campsite.
- **Clock / focus mode** — a visible clock or focus timer helps study and work sessions, not just sleep.
