CCleaner is well known, but many users want either lighter cleaning or real monitoring. These **alternatives to CCleaner** cover both — and we'll be honest about what cleaners can and can't do.

![CCleaner alternatives overview](/assets/images/apps/system-monitor/screen-alt.webp)

## The best CCleaner alternatives

| App | Focus | Clean | Monitor | Price |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **System Monitor** | Monitor + boost | Storage overview | **Yes (live)** | Free core + unlock |
| **Files by Google** | Safe storage | **Yes (official)** | No | Free |
| **SD Maid** | Deep cleaner | **Yes (deep)** | Partial | Free + Pro |
| **AVG Cleaner** | Clean + photos | Yes | No | Free (ads) |

### 1. System Monitor — best for monitoring

An **all-in-one system monitor** with CPU, RAM boost, battery doctor, storage overview, and overheat protection. Great if you want insight, not just cleanup.

### 2. Files by Google — best safe cleaner

Google's official tool for clearing unused files, duplicates, and managing storage, with a privacy-friendly design and no aggressive background tasks.

### 3. SD Maid — best deep cleaner

A thorough cleaning toolkit for caches and leftovers (some features paid). Strong when you truly need deep cleanup.

### 4. AVG Cleaner — best photo cleaner

Focuses on cleaning and photo optimization to free space.

## A note on cleaners

On modern Android, the system already manages RAM and cache. Repeated force-closing can **cost battery** as apps reopen. So:
- Use **monitoring** to understand what uses resources (System Monitor).
- Use **safe, occasional cleanup** for genuine junk (Files by Google).
- Reserve **deep cleaning** for when you really need it (SD Maid).

## Which CCleaner alternative fits you?

- Want **monitoring and boost** → **System Monitor**
- Want safe official cleanup → Files by Google
- Want deep cleaning → SD Maid
- Want photo space saving → AVG Cleaner
