When your phone gets hot or sluggish, you want to know why — not just a vague 'optimizing' message. A CPU monitor shows the numbers behind the slowdown, and this 2026 guide covers what to look for and how CPU Monitor compares.

## Why CPU temperature monitor matters on Android

On Android, a status-bar and desktop floating window plus home-screen widgets show live temperatures without opening the app — ideal while gaming or charging, when heat actually spikes. Background monitoring is light enough to leave on all the time.

## What to look for in a CPU temperature monitor

- **Real-time temperature** — actual CPU and battery temperature, not a guess from load.
- **Frequency & multicore** — per-core speed and load so you see throttling.
- **Floating window & widgets** — live stats above any app and on the home screen.
- **Overheat alarm** — a warning before heat damages battery or performance.
- **History & analysis** — temperature/frequency curves to spot what triggers spikes.
- **Lightweight** — monitoring shouldn't itself drain the battery or slow the phone.
