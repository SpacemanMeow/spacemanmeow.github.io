Streaks is a favorite **habit tracker** on iOS, but if you need Android support, a free tier, or break-a-habit tracking, these **alternatives to Streaks** are worth a look in 2026.

![Streaks alternatives overview](/assets/images/apps/meow-habits/screen-alt.webp)

## Why people look beyond Streaks

- **iOS-only** — no Android app.
- **Paid one-time** (~$4.99) — no free tier to try first.
- **Build-focused** — weaker for "quit a habit" goals.
- Some want **cross-platform** sync (phone + tablet).

## The best Streaks alternatives

| App | Price | Platforms | Build + Break | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Meow Habits** | Free core + VIP | Android, iOS | **Yes** | Privacy, charts, cute UI |
| **Loop** | Free, open-source | Android | Yes | Plain stats, no account |
| **Habitica** | Free + sub (~$48/yr) | Android, iOS, web | Yes | Gamification (RPG) |
| **Fabulous** | Free + sub (~$12/mo) | Android, iOS | Routines + coaching | Guided coaching |

### 1. Meow Habits — best cross-platform pick

A **cute habit tracker** for Android and iOS with build **and** break modes, streaks, charts, offline privacy, fingerprint lock, and CSV export. Core tracking is free.

### 2. Loop — best plain-stats Android pick

A long-standing, **free, open-source** Android **habit tracker** with targets, reminders, and detailed stats. No account, no upsell — just tracking.

### 3. Fabulous — best for coaching

More than tracking — Fabulous adds guided routines and science-backed coaching behind a subscription, good if you want hand-holding.

### 4. Habitica — best for gamification

Turns habits into an RPG. Great if you stay motivated by games (free with optional subscription).

## Which Streaks alternative fits you?

- Want **free and cross-platform** → **Meow Habits**
- Want **coaching** → Fabulous
- Want **games** → Habitica
- Want **plain free Android stats** → Loop

## How to migrate away from Streaks

1. **List your current Streaks tasks** and their schedules.
2. **Pick a replacement** using the table above based on platform and goal.
3. **Recreate habits** — pick build or break mode where supported.
4. **Set reminders** for the same times you used in Streaks.
5. **Enable backup** (Meow Habits: Google Drive / WebDav) so you're never locked in.
