## Why overheating happens

- **Heavy use** — gaming, 4K video, GPS navigation push CPU/GPU.
- **Environment** — direct sunlight or a hot car.
- **Signal** — poor reception makes the modem transmit harder.
- **Battery** — an aging battery runs hot under load.

## What actually cools it down

1. **Close heavy apps** or use one-tap boost.
2. **Remove the case** and set the phone down on a cool surface.
3. **Avoid direct sun** and pause charging while gaming.
4. **Lower screen brightness** and turn off GPS/Bluetooth when unused.
5. Let it **rest** — no app cools hardware; detection and action do.
