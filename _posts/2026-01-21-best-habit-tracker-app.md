Building habits is easier when the app makes consistency satisfying. **Meow Habits** is a **cute habit tracker** for Android and iOS that turns routines into streaks, charts, and small daily wins. Below we compare the apps people actually install — **Meow Habits, Streaks, Habitica, Fabulous, and Loop** — across build/break modes, price, platforms, and privacy.

## What makes a great habit tracker

A strong **habit tracker app** should help you:
- **Build *or* break** habits (not just one direction).
- Show **streaks** and longest runs.
- Visualize with **charts and calendars**.
- **Remind** you daily without nagging.
- Keep your data **private**.

## Meow Habits in depth

From the app itself:

- **Build or Break** habits (form a routine or quit one).
- Periods: daily, weekly, monthly, weekdays.
- Targets by **count or time**, with daily limits.
- **Streak**, longest run, and average completion.
- Grouped lists (health, workout, study, productivity, saving, happiness) plus a template library.
- Trends (completion rate, quantity pie, achievements).
- **CSV export**, daily reminders, achievement sharing.
- **Fingerprint lock** and offline privacy.
- **500+ cute icons** and dark/light mode.
- Google Drive / WebDav backup.

![Meow Habits trends](/assets/images/apps/meow-habits/screen-trends.webp)

**Where it wins**
- Cross-platform (Android + iOS) with offline-first privacy.
- Both build and break modes.
- Rich charts plus CSV export — rare in free tiers.

**Where it's lighter**
- No RPG gamification (unlike Habitica) or coaching journeys (unlike Fabulous).

## How the others compare

- **Streaks** is the polished iOS-only minimalist; you pay once but Android users are left out.
- **Habitica** turns habits into an RPG — motivating if you like games, distracting if you don't.
- **Fabulous** adds science-backed coaching routines behind a subscription.
- **Loop** is a free, open-source Android tracker focused purely on stats, no account needed.

## How to start with Meow Habits in 4 steps

1. Pick a template (for example "Drink water").
2. Set a target (3 times a day).
3. Check in daily and watch the **streak** grow.
4. Review the monthly **trends**.

- Want **free, cross-platform, private** → **Meow Habits**.
