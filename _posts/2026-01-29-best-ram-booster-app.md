## How Android actually uses RAM

Modern Android keeps **recently used apps in RAM on purpose** — reopening them is instant. "Free RAM" isn't always better; the OS evicts apps when it needs memory. A manual boost helps mainly:
- right after a **heavy session** (game, video call),
- when an app feels stuck,
- to **close a runaway background app**.

So treat boost as a tool, not a habit — and prefer monitoring to understand *what* eats memory.
