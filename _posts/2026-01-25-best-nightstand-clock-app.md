## Set it up in 3 steps

1. Place a charged phone on your nightstand (keep it plugged in).
2. Open Only Clock, pick a dark theme (black or space).
3. Enable **white noise** and lower the brightness.

## Tips to protect your screen

- **Stay plugged in** — a nightstand clock runs all night.
- **Lower brightness** so it's readable but not harsh.
- **Enable anti-burn-in** / clock-shift if offered; Only Clock's dark themes and Retroflip's setting both help prevent OLED ghosting.
- **Turn off seconds** if you don't need them — calmer and saves a little power.
