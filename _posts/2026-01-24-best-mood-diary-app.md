A **mood diary** helps you notice patterns in how you feel. **Meow Mood** is a **private mood tracker** for Android and iOS that makes logging gentle: tap an emotion, add context, and watch the trends. Below we compare the apps people actually install — **Meow Mood, Day One, Journey, Daylio, and Grid Diary** — across privacy, mood tracking, price, and platforms.

![Meow Mood logging](/assets/images/apps/meow-mood-diary/screen-log.webp)

## What the best journaling app should do

A good **mood tracker** or **journaling app** should let you:
- Record feelings in seconds.
- Tag **context** (work, family, health, money).
- See **trends** over time.
- Stay **private**.

## Meow Mood in depth

From the app itself:

- **5-level mood** scale plus detailed emotion tags (anger, anxiety, calm, happy, and more).
- **Context tags** — work, family, health, money, weather, partner.
- Short text notes, timestamped.
- **Day / week / month** statistics and mood distribution.
- **PDF export** and daily reminders.
- **Fingerprint or passcode lock**.
- Fully **offline** and never uploaded.
- Cute cat theme, multiple color themes, Google Drive backup.

![Meow Mood trends](/assets/images/apps/meow-mood-diary/screen-trends.webp)

**Where it wins**
- Privacy-first: nothing leaves your device by default.
- Fast, emotion-focused logging — no pressure to write paragraphs.
- PDF export for reflection or sharing with a therapist.

**Where it's lighter**
- No photos/audio (it's a mood tracker, not a multimedia life journal).
- Less rich prose tooling than Day One or Journey.

## How the others compare

- **Day One** is the multimedia life journal — photos, audio, video, and cloud sync, behind a ~$35/year subscription.
- **Journey** is the closest Day One alternative with broad platform support and cloud sync.
- **Daylio** is micro-journaling: pick a mood and activities in two taps, great for speed.
- **Grid Diary** uses guided prompts to beat blank-page paralysis.

## How to start with Meow Mood in 4 steps

1. Each day, tap "How are you feeling?".
2. Pick a mood level and emotion.
3. Add a context tag and a short note.
4. Review **weekly and monthly** trends.

## Which should you pick?

- Want **privacy + quick mood tracking** → **Meow Mood**.
- Want a **multimedia life journal** → Day One.
- Want **cross-platform prose journaling** → Journey.
- Want **two-tap speed** → Daylio.
