## What beginners actually need

A habit tracker for beginners in 2026 needs six things: a one-tap start with no long form, a template library for habits like drinking water three times a day, room for just one or two habits at first, gentle reminders that nudge rather than nag, a free core to prove the routine, and a forgiving streak that does not erase progress after a missed day. Meow Habits is built around those six; the four contenders below are weighed on how fast a first check-in happens.

- **One-tap start** — no long setup, no 20-field form.
- **A template library** — "drink water 3× a day" in a single tap.
- **One or two habits** — start small; adding ten at once is how streaks die.
- **Gentle reminders** — nudge, don't nag.
- **Free core** — prove the habit before paying.
- **A forgiving streak** — a missed day shouldn't erase everything.
