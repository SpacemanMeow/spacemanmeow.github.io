Sometimes you want controls on top of any app. **Floating Assistant** is a **floating assistant app** that combines assistive touch, a floating clock, and a floating system monitor in one overlay. Below we compare the tools people actually use — **Floating Assistant, Assistive Touch (Easy Touch), Floating Apps, and Easy Touch** — across features and price.

![Floating Assistant overview](/assets/images/apps/floating-assistant/screen-overview.webp)

## What the best overlay tool should do

A strong **floating window** tool should offer:
- **Assistive touch** for navigation without hardware keys
- A **floating clock** you can move anywhere
- A **floating monitor** for CPU, RAM, and battery
- Quick shortcuts and privacy respect

## The contenders at a glance

| App | Assistive touch | Floating clock | Floating monitor | Extras | Price |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Floating Assistant** | **Yes** | **Yes** | **Yes (CPU/RAM/battery)** | Clock + monitor combo | Free core + unlock |
| **Assistive Touch (Easy Touch)** | **Yes** | No | No | Virtual buttons, gestures | Free (ads) |
| **Floating Apps** | Launcher | Yes (mini apps) | No | Floating mini-apps | Free + IAP |

## Floating Assistant in depth

From the app itself:

- **Assistive Touch** — virtual home, back, recent, volume; one-tap boost, lock screen, screenshot, flashlight, rotation, auto brightness, quick app launch.
- **Floating clock and countdown** — draggable, 24-hour and seconds, position saved, works over fullscreen apps.
- **Floating monitor** — real-time CPU temp, frequency, usage, RAM, battery with curves and one-tap boost (great for gaming).
- Accessibility-based, with a stated no-collection privacy approach.

![Floating Assistant monitor](/assets/images/apps/floating-assistant/screen-monitor.webp)

**Where it wins**
- Three floating modules in one app — buttons, clock, and live monitor.
- The **floating monitor** is unique here: live CPU/RAM/battery while gaming.
- Good for big phones and worn hardware buttons.

**Where it's lighter**
- It's an overlay utility, not a full automation engine (use Tasker for deep automation).

## How the others compare

- **Assistive Touch (Easy Touch)** is the classic free floating ball: virtual buttons, customizable size/color/gestures, but no clock or monitor.
- **Floating Apps** launches mini floating apps (notes, calculator, browser) over anything.
- **Easy Touch** is an iOS-style floating ball focused on navigation.

## How to start in 3 steps

1. Grant the overlay and accessibility permissions.
2. Enable **assistive touch** and the **floating clock**.
3. Turn on the **floating monitor** for live stats.

## Which should you pick?

- Want **buttons + clock + monitor** → **Floating Assistant**.
- Want **just navigation buttons** → Assistive Touch (Easy Touch).
- Want **mini floating apps** → Floating Apps.
