One Widget is an **all-in-one Android home-screen toolkit** packing 500+ premium widgets across four trending styles: Glass iOS, Material You, Galaxy One UI, and Nothing minimalist. You pick a finished widget, resize it, and you're done.

### Strengths
- **500+ ready-made widgets** — no editor, no design skills; tap and place.
- **Four popular styles** — Glass, Material You, Galaxy One UI, Nothing — covering most aesthetic tastes.
- **8 functional categories**: app shortcuts, system quick toggles (Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, torch…), device hardware info (battery, RAM, storage), clock/calendar, notes/quotes, contacts, aggregated app launchers (Google/AI/social), and mini tools (compass, pedometer, calculator).
- **Full customization of size** (1x1 to 2x6), icon background color and corner radius to match your theme.
- **Quick toggles and app shortcuts** as home-screen widgets — genuinely useful, not just decorative.
- **Low learning curve** — ideal for non-designers wanting a polished desktop fast.
- **Free One UI set**; other styles unlock with a one-time purchase.

### Weaknesses
- **Android only** — no iOS version.
- **Preset-bound** — you customize within finished designs, not a blank canvas.
- **Limited live-data depth** — widgets are curated; you can't bind arbitrary formulas or exotic data sources.
- **Export/sharing is limited** compared with a true builder.
- Other style families require a purchase (One UI is free).

### Best for
Users who want a cohesive, good-looking home screen quickly without learning a design tool.

KWGT (Kustom Widget Maker) is the most powerful widget builder on Android. You place text, shapes, images and data points, then bind them to live sources — weather, music, battery, or your own formulas — to create entirely original widgets.

### Strengths
- **Unlimited customization** — every element (position, size, font, color, layer) is yours to define.
- **Full live-data binding** — weather, music, system stats, and custom formulas via Kustom's expression language.
- **Bespoke layouts** — recreate any look (or invent your own) down to the pixel.
- **Large community** with shared presets and tutorials.
- **Pro key (~$4.99)** removes the watermark and unlocks full export/sharing.
- **Free to use** at the core for building and testing.

### Weaknesses
- **High learning curve** — placing and binding elements takes real effort; not for casual users.
- **Time investment** — a great widget can take hours versus One Widget's seconds.
- **Android only**, no iOS.
- **Watermark and limited export** until you buy the Pro key.
- Can feel overwhelming if you "just want a nice clock widget."

### Best for
Designers and tweakers who want a one-of-a-kind home screen and enjoy building it themselves.

**Download KWGT**
- [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=org.kustom.widget)

## How they compare where it matters

### Speed vs control
One Widget wins on time-to-beautiful: pick a preset, resize, done. KWGT wins on control: a blank canvas where you dictate every pixel and data source. If you value your time, One Widget; if you value uniqueness, KWGT.

### Live data
Both can show live info, but the depth differs. One Widget's widgets are curated (clock, battery, RAM, toggles). KWGT lets you bind *any* data — weather formulas, now-playing music metadata, custom calculations — into custom layouts. For exotic data, KWGT is unmatched.

### Aesthetics
One Widget guarantees a cohesive, trendy look (Glass/One UI/Nothing/Material You) because professionals designed the presets. KWGT's output depends entirely on your skill — amazing or amateur.

### Price
One Widget is free for One UI with a one-time unlock for other families. KWGT is free to build, with a ~$4.99 Pro key for watermark removal and export. Both are reasonable; neither is subscription-heavy.

## Which should you choose

- **Choose One Widget** if you want a polished, cohesive home screen in minutes and prefer curated styles over a design tool.
- **Choose KWGT** if you want total creative control, live-data formulas, and don't mind learning (and spending time) to build unique widgets.
- **Many Android users keep both**: One Widget for fast, everyday widgets, and KWGT when they want something truly custom.

## Other widget makers worth considering

A two-app comparison can't cover every reason people look for a **KWGT alternative**. The right substitute depends on what you were trying to build:

- A **Kustom Widget Maker alternative** is for people who liked KWGT's power but not its editor: ready presets you resize, not layers you draw. One Widget is the short path here.
- A **Chronus alternative** is usually about a dependable clock/calendar/news widget with lots of typography options; if that is all you need, a general-purpose pack may be more than you want.
- A **Widgetopia alternative** or **Zooper Widget alternative** is the search when you want a big preset library rather than a scripting tool — the same reason the **best KWGT alternative android** shortlists tend to include ready-made packs.
- A **Widgetsmith alternative** fits iOS-style layouts; on Android, One Widget's Glass presets cover that look without an iPhone-only app.

## Typical scenarios

- **You want a polished home screen tonight** — One Widget's 500+ ready widgets mean pick, resize, done; KWGT would need a build session first.
- **You love building from scratch** — KWGT's editor and Kode let you bind weather, music and custom formulas into a one-of-a-kind widget.
- **You just need quick toggles** — One Widget ships Wi-Fi/Bluetooth/torch and app shortcuts as widgets; no scripting required.
- **You want exotic live data** — KWGT's full data binding is unmatched for now-playing, weather formulas and bespoke stats.
- **Keep both** — many Android users run One Widget for everyday widgets and KWGT when they want something truly custom.

## FAQ

**One Widget or KWGT — which should I pick?**
{:.faq-q}

Want a cohesive, polished home screen in minutes, no editor → One Widget; want total creative control with live-data formulas and don't mind the learning curve → KWGT.

**Is One Widget free?**
{:.faq-q}

The One UI style set is free; the other three styles unlock with a one-time purchase (no subscription).

**Does One Widget work on iOS?**
{:.faq-q}

No. One Widget is Android-only; KWGT is also Android-only. For iOS, look at WidgetSmith.

**Is KWGT subscription-free?**
{:.faq-q}

KWGT is free to build and test; a ~$4.99 one-time Pro key removes the watermark and unlocks full export. No subscription.
