A great home screen starts with the right **widgets**. **One Widget** is an **Android widgets app** that bundles 500+ curated widgets across four 2026 design languages — One UI, Nothing, Glass, and Material You. Below we compare the apps people actually install — **One Widget, KWGT, Color Widgets, and WidgetClub** — across ready styles, customization, and price.

![One Widget library](/assets/images/apps/one-widget/screen-library.webp)

## What the best widget app should offer

A top **home screen customization** tool should provide:
- Multiple cohesive **design styles**.
- Clock, calendar, and system info widgets.
- **Quick settings toggles**.
- App shortcuts and contacts.
- Resizable layouts.

## The contenders at a glance

| App | Price (2026) | Approach | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **One Widget** | Free One UI + one-time unlock | 500+ ready widgets, 4 styles | Polished, themed sets |
| **KWGT** | Free + Pro key (~$4.99) | Build-from-scratch editor | Total custom design |
| **Color Widgets** | Free + IAP/subscription | Template editor | Quick themed widgets |
| **WidgetClub** | Free + IAP | Pre-designed themes | Cute aesthetic sets |

## One Widget in depth

From the app itself:

- **500+ widgets** in 4 styles: One UI, Nothing, Glass, Material You.
- **Hardware widgets** — battery (level, temp, health), CPU usage/temp/frequency, RAM, storage.
- **Quick toggles** — Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, flashlight, rotation, airplane, hotspot, DND, location, vibration, dark mode.
- **App launcher, contacts, notes, tools** (compass, pedometer, tap counter, calculator).
- **Clock and calendar** widgets.
- Resizable 1x1 to 2x6, in-widget settings.

![One Widget styles](/assets/images/apps/one-widget/screen-styles.webp)

**Where it wins**
- Four on-trend styles in one app — no mixing multiple widget apps.
- Battery, RAM, and toggle widgets work immediately, no editor.
- Free One UI set to try first.

**Where it's lighter**
- Not a from-scratch editor — for fully bespoke layouts, KWGT is deeper.

## How the others compare

- **KWGT** is the power editor: design any widget with code-free (mostly) modules; free with a one-time Pro key.
- **Color Widgets** offers a template editor for quick themed widgets, free with in-app purchases.
- **WidgetClub** focuses on cute, pre-designed aesthetic sets.

## How to start with One Widget in 3 steps

1. Pick a style (One UI, Nothing, Glass, or Material You).
2. Choose a widget (clock, battery, toggle, shortcut).
3. Add it to your home screen and configure (for example step goal).

## Which should you pick?

- Want **ready, cohesive widgets** → **One Widget**.
- Want **total custom design** → KWGT.
- Want **quick themed templates** → Color Widgets.
