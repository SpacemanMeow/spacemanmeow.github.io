Between lectures, assignments, and part-time shifts, students need a **task manager** that is fast and free. - **Free core** — you shouldn't pay to track homework.

- **Calendar view** — see every course deadline on one screen.
- **No account required** — one less password during week one.
- **Export** — CSV to hand a plan to a group mate or print it.

Meow Todo is a **cat-themed to-do list** for Android and iOS. The core — lists, tasks, reminders, and calendar view — is free; VIP unlocks unlimited lists, tags, subtasks, CSV export, and app lock.

**Strengths for students**
- **Free core** — lists, tasks, due dates, and a calendar view cost nothing.
- **No account required** — data sits on your device behind a fingerprint lock.
- **Subtasks** — split an assignment into milestones so nothing slips.
- **Reminders** — daily and due-task notifications keep the semester visible.
- **Backup you control** — Google Drive or WebDav, only when you enable it.
- **CSV export** — hand a plan to a group mate or keep a printable copy.

**Weaknesses for students**
- **VIP for power features** — unlimited lists, tags, and CSV export sit behind the upgrade.
- **No auto cloud sync** — cross-device happens through your own Drive/WebDav, not a live account.

**Best for:** budget-tight, privacy-minded students who want a **free task manager** with no sign-up.

Todoist is the most-installed task manager around, trusted by 30 million users, with natural-language input ("submit essay tomorrow 5pm") and strong collaboration.

**Strengths for students**
- **Natural language** — type a task and it parses the date and priority.
- **Projects and subtasks** — organize by course, with nested steps.
- **Cross-platform + sharing** — share a project list with group mates.
- **Karma/limits** — gentle streaks that reward consistency.

**Weaknesses for students**
- **Free tier is capped** — limited projects, comments, and labels; Pro (~$48/yr) unlocks the rest.
- **Cloud account required** — your tasks live on Todoist's servers.
- **Cost adds up** — a paid tier every year on a student budget.

**Best for:** students who want a method, collaboration, and will pay for Pro.

- [Google Play — Todoist](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.todoist)
- [App Store — Todoist](https://apps.apple.com/app/id572688855)

## How the others compare

- **Microsoft To Do** is completely free inside the Microsoft world, with shared lists and Outlook integration — ideal if your school uses Microsoft 365.
- **TickTick** bundles tasks, a calendar, a pomodoro timer, and habit tracking; free with a subscription for the full set.

## Which should a student pick?

- Want **free + private + no account** → **Meow Todo**.
- Want **natural-language + collaboration** and accept Pro → **Todoist**.
- Live in **Microsoft 365** → **Microsoft To Do**.
- Want **one app for tasks, calendar, and focus** → **TickTick**.
