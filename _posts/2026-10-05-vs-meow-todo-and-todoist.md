This **Meow Todo vs Todoist** comparison helps you pick the **to-do list app** that matches how you actually work, with 2026 pricing and real feature limits.

Meow Todo is a **simple schedule planner and to-do list manager** with a clean, cute interface. Its standout trait is the friendly design — 400+ cartoon icons and a calming look — plus practical essentials: subtasks, a calendar view, reminders, and CSV export, all in a free core.

### Strengths
- **Cute, clean design** with **400+ cartoon icons** for lists and tags — logging feels friendly, not clinical.
- **Calendar view** — daily, weekly and monthly planners for a clear schedule overview.
- **Subtasks and details** — break tasks down and add context as work progresses.
- **Reminders** to keep tasks from slipping.
- **Privacy-first** — tasks stay on your device with an optional fingerprint lock; sync to your own Google Drive or WebDav.
- **CSV export** of tasks for Excel review.
- **Free core** (lists, tasks, reminders, subtasks, calendar, export) with optional VIP for extras.
- **Charts** (pie, contribution graph) to track task activity.

### Weaknesses
- **No natural-language dates** — you pick dates manually rather than typing "every mon 9am".
- **No collaboration** — personal lists only; no shared projects.
- **No web app** — mobile-focused.
- **VIP required** for some advanced features.
- Less powerful filtering/labeling than Todoist's power-user toolkit.

### Best for
Individuals who want a private, cute, straightforward task list with a calendar view and export — no account, no team features needed.

Todoist is one of the most popular task managers worldwide: natural-language input, powerful labels and filters, and collaboration via shared projects. It scales from a personal to-do to a team workflow.

### Strengths
- **Natural-language input** — type "Submit report every Mon 9am" and dates/recurrence are parsed automatically.
- **Powerful organization** — projects, labels, priorities and filters for complex workflows.
- **Collaboration** — shared projects and comments (Pro) for teams and families.
- **Cross-platform** with a mature web app and deep integrations (calendar, Slack, etc.).
- **Recurring and reminders** with flexible scheduling.
- **Large ecosystem** and constant updates.

### Weaknesses
- **Free tier is limited** — roughly 5 active projects, restricted reminders, no calendar integration; heavy users need Pro (~$60/yr).
- **Cloud account required** — data lives in Todoist's servers, not local-only.
- **Steeper setup** for simple users who just want a list.
- **No CSV export** of tasks in the standard tiers.
- **Less "cute"** — utilitarian UI, no cartoon icons.

### Best for
Power users and teams who want natural-language capture, rich filtering, and shared projects across web and mobile.

## How they compare where it matters

### Simplicity vs power
Meow Todo is "open and list" — ideal for personal planning without a learning curve. Todoist is a power tool: labels, filters, and natural-language capture reward investment but add setup.

### Natural-language capture
This is Todoist's headline edge: "every mon 9am" just works. Meow Todo uses manual date picking, which is fine but slower for recurring, complex schedules.

### Collaboration
Todoist supports shared projects and comments (Pro) — essential for teams. Meow Todo is personal-only. If you plan with others, Todoist is the clear pick.

### Privacy and sync
Meow Todo is offline-first with fingerprint lock and your own Drive/WebDav backups. Todoist is cloud-first. For sensitive or local-only task data, Meow Todo gives more control.

### Price and limits
Meow Todo's core is free with no hard project cap. Todoist's free Beginner tier caps at ~5 projects; Pro unlocks the rest at ~$60/year. Meow Todo is the lower-friction, lower-cost option for individuals.

## Which should you choose

- **Choose Meow Todo** if you want a private, cute, simple to-do list with a calendar view, subtasks and CSV export — no team features or account required.
- **Choose Todoist** if you need natural-language capture, powerful filters, and shared projects for collaboration, and will pay for Pro.
- **For personal, privacy-minded planning**, Meow Todo covers the essentials for free; **for team or power workflows**, Todoist justifies its subscription.
