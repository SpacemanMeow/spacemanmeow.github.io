It pairs a cat-themed interface with bar, pie, calendar and trend charts, offline logging behind a fingerprint lock, and sync to your own Google Drive or WebDav. ## Streaks, in detail

Streaks is an iOS-only minimalist habit app built around completing up to 12 positive tasks a day and watching the chain grow. It integrates with HealthKit, Siri, widgets, Apple Watch and Shortcuts, syncs through iCloud across iPhone, iPad and Mac, and costs a one-time ~$4.99 with no subscription. It is intentionally build-only: no break mode, a limited system-icon style, no CSV export and no Android version.

Streaks is the minimalist iOS habit app that helped define the category. You pick up to 12 tasks, mark them done each day, and watch your chain grow. It's all about focus and Apple-ecosystem polish.

### Strengths
- **Beautifully minimal** — a clean, award-winning iOS interface that gets out of the way.
- **Deep Apple integration** — HealthKit, Siri, widgets, Apple Watch and Shortcuts.
- **Up to 12 tasks** with smart reminders and flexible schedules.
- **iCloud sync** across iPhone, iPad and Mac, seamless for Apple users.
- **One-time purchase** — no subscription.
- **Motivating streak mechanics** that keep you coming back.

### Weaknesses
- **iOS only** — no Android app at all.
- **Mostly build mode** — designed for completing positive tasks, not explicitly breaking habits.
- **Limited icon/customization** — system icons and a restrained style (great for some, plain for others).
- **No CSV export** of your history in the standard experience.
- **Capped at 12 tasks** — fine for focus, limiting for power users.
- **iCloud-only** sync ties you to Apple's ecosystem.

### Best for
iPhone-only users who want a beautifully simple, focused tracker and don't need to break bad habits or use Android.

## How they compare where it matters

Meow Habits and Streaks both reward daily check-ins, but five dimensions separate them. Meow Habits runs on Android and iOS, supports both build and break modes with 500+ icons and four chart types, and keeps data offline behind a fingerprint lock with your own Drive/WebDav backup. Streaks is iOS-only, capped at 12 tasks, and leans on HealthKit, Watch and Shortcuts with iCloud sync. On price, Meow Habits is free at the core while Streaks is a ~$4.99 one-time buy.

### Platforms
This is decisive for many. Meow Habits runs on Android and iOS; Streaks is iOS-only. If anyone in your household or workflow is on Android, Meow Habits is the only option of the two.

### Build vs break
Streaks is built around *doing* positive tasks. Meow Habits explicitly supports *breaking* habits too — quitting smoking, less screen time, earlier nights — which Streaks doesn't model as naturally.

### Privacy and sync
Meow Habits is offline-first with a fingerprint lock and sync to *your* Google Drive/WebDav. Streaks uses iCloud. Both are reasonable; Meow Habits gives you more direct control and local-only use.

### Customization and charts
Meow Habits wins on icon variety (500+) and chart types (bars, pies, calendar, trends) plus CSV export. Streaks wins on restraint and Apple-native widgets/Shortcuts.

### Price
Meow Habits is free at the core; Streaks is a ~$4.99 one-time buy. Neither is subscription-based.

## Which should you choose

Meow Habits suits you if you use Android or both platforms, want to track breaking habits, love 500+ cute icons, or prefer offline/private tracking with CSV export; Streaks suits you if you're iPhone-only, want the most polished minimal tracker and live inside Apple's ecosystem. Neither uses a subscription. For most cross-platform or privacy-minded users Meow Habits covers more cases; for a pure-iOS simplicity fan Streaks remains the cleaner pick.

## FAQ

**Meow Habits or Streaks — which should I pick?**
{:.faq-q}

Want Android or both platforms, build-and-break modes, cute icons, and offline/private tracking → Meow Habits; want the most polished minimal iOS-only tracker inside Apple's ecosystem → Streaks.

**Is Meow Habits free?**
{:.faq-q}

Yes for the core — streaks, charts, reminders, and templates are free; CSV export and a few extras sit behind an optional one-time VIP.

**Does Meow Habits work offline?**
{:.faq-q}

Yes. You can log habits with no internet and no account; data stays on your device and backs up to Google Drive or WebDav when you choose.

**Can Streaks break bad habits?**
{:.faq-q}

Not naturally — Streaks is designed around completing positive tasks; Meow Habits explicitly supports both build and break modes.
