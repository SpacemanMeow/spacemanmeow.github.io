Heat isn't only about the CPU — the battery, GPU, and ambient sensors all contribute, and a hot battery ages faster. A **phone temperature app** should read the whole device, warn you, and suggest a fix. **CPU Monitor** is a **device temperature monitor for Android** with alerts and advice. Below we compare it with **CPU-Z** for managing the device's overall heat.

## What a phone temperature app needs

- **Multiple heat sources** — CPU plus battery and sensor reads.
- **Overheat alerts** — a nudge to put the phone down.
- **Plain advice** — "close games, unplug, cool down."
- **Light monitoring** — no extra heat from the app itself.

## CPU Monitor, in depth

CPU Monitor is a **device temperature monitor** for Android covering CPU, battery, and sensor heat, with alerts and suggestions.

**Strengths for device heat**
- **Multiple sources** — watch CPU and battery temperature together.
- **Overheat alerts** — warned before the battery takes damage.
- **Actionable advice** — the app suggests cooling steps, not just numbers.
- **Lightweight** — low background cost.

**Weaknesses for device heat**
- **VIP for deeper logs** — long history behind the upgrade.
- **Sensor dependence** — richness varies by device.

**Best for:** people who want to protect the whole phone from heat, not just read one number.

- [Google Play — CPU Monitor](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.glgjing.stark)


## CPU-Z, in depth

CPU-Z (package `com.cpuid.cpu_z`) reports hardware details including current temperatures from available sensors.

**Strengths for device heat**
- **Free and detailed** — SoC, battery, and sensor data in one screen.
- **Trusted accuracy** — CPUID's spec reporting is solid.

**Weaknesses for device heat**
- **No alerts** — it shows the temp, it won't warn you.
- **No advice** — raw numbers, no "what to do."
- **Limited mobile sensors** — the Android build is lighter than the PC tool.

**Best for:** users who want a free spec-and-temp snapshot.

- [Google Play — CPU-Z](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.cpuid.cpu_z)

## How the others compare

- **DevCheck** adds battery and live device info for free; solid, but still no proactive alerts.

## Which should you pick?

- Want **whole-device heat + alerts + advice** → **CPU Monitor**.
- Want **free specs and a current temp** → **CPU-Z**.
