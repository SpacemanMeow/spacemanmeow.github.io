## Try these for sleep

- **Rain** — steady and soothing.
- **Sea** — gentle waves.
- **Fireplace** — warm and cozy.
- **Summer night** — crickets and calm.

## Why these sounds help

- **Masking** — steady noise covers abrupt sounds (traffic, snoring) that wake you.
- **Consistency** — a constant bedside sound cues your brain that it's time to sleep.
- **No lyrics** — unlike music, noise won't pull your attention.

## Tips for better sleep

1. **Set a 30–60 min timer** so sound stops after you're asleep.
2. **Keep volume low** — just enough to mask, not to刺激.
3. **Pair with a dark room** and consistent bedtime.
4. **Try a week of one sound** before switching, to learn what works.
