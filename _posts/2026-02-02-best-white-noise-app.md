A good **white noise app** can be the difference between a restless night and deep sleep — or between a distracted afternoon and focused work. **Meow FM** is a **cute white noise player** for Android and iOS with soundscapes for sleep, relax, and concentrate. Below we compare the apps people actually install — **Meow FM, Calm, Noisli, myNoise, and White Noise (TMSOFT)** — across sounds, mixing, timers, price, and platforms.

![Meow FM player](/assets/images/apps/meow-fm/screen-player.webp)

## What the best sleep sounds app should do

A strong **white noise for sleep** app should:
- Offer a variety of natural soundscapes.
- Include a **sleep timer** that auto-stops.
- Work in the background.
- Be simple to use at bedtime.

## The contenders at a glance

| App | Price (2026) | Sound mixing | Timer | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Meow FM** | Free core + unlock | No (curated scenes) | Yes (auto-stop) | Cute, simple sleep/focus |
| **Calm** | ~$69.99/yr | Music + soundscapes | Yes | Meditation + sleep stories |
| **Noisli** | ~$60/yr | **Yes (mix layers)** | Yes | Work focus |
| **myNoise** | Free + supporter | **Yes (deep tuning)** | Yes | Precise sound design |
| **White Noise (TMSOFT)** | ~$1.99 one-time | Loops + mixes | Yes | Huge sound library |

## Meow FM in depth

From the app itself:

- **Many soundscapes** — rain, thunder, wind, forest birds, flowing water, sea, fireplace, summer night.
- **Timer auto-stop** so playback ends on its own.
- **Widgets** — single sound, list, and player cover.
- **Desktop clock** for focus sessions.
- Dark and light modes.
- A **cute cat-themed** calming design.
- Free core sounds, additional soundscapes as an upgrade.

![Meow FM widget](/assets/images/apps/meow-fm/screen-widget.webp)

**Where it wins**
- One tap to play — no lessons, no account.
- Cute, calming design that suits bedtime.
- Free core sounds plus a sleep timer.

**Where it's lighter**
- No sound *mixing* or fine-grained tuning (use Noisli or myNoise for that).
- Not a meditation/sleep-story library (that's Calm's turf).

## How the others compare

- **Calm** is a full wellness subscription — guided meditation, sleep stories, and music — at ~$69.99/year.
- **Noisli** lets you mix layers (rain + wind + cafe) for a custom focus blend.
- **myNoise** offers deeply adjustable generators engineered for sleep, relax, and tinnitus.
- **White Noise (TMSOFT)** ships a large loop library, mostly a one-time purchase.

## How to start with Meow FM in 3 steps

1. Pick a soundscape (for example "Rain").
2. Press play and relax.
3. Set a **timer** so it stops while you sleep.

## Which should you pick?

- Want **simple sounds + timer, free** → **Meow FM**.
- Want **meditation + stories** → Calm.
- Want **mix your own blend** → Noisli or myNoise.
- Want a **big library** → White Noise (TMSOFT).
