## What people call it

One small ball accumulates a lot of names, and each label signals a slightly different job:

- **Floating assistant** / **android floating assistant** / **floating ball** / **floating button** — the overlay itself. A **best floating ball android** shortlist is usually about how it looks and where it docks.
- **Assistive touch** / **best assistive touch app android** — borrowed from the iPhone's on-screen home button; on Android people usually mean a floating menu of system actions.
- **Edge floating ball** — a ball that parks on the screen edge and expands on swipe, which is where **edge floating ball with gestures** comes from.
- **One-hand helper** — the reason people install it: a **floating button for one hand** makes a tall phone reachable without shifting your grip.
- **Customizable floating ball android** — a request for your own shortcuts, contacts and toggles instead of a fixed menu.

What separates the apps below is not the name but three things: whether the ball stays put across apps without root, whether long-press and swipe can be assigned to real actions, and whether it stays light in the background. Floating Assistant covers those with an edge-docked ball; Assistive Touch is the minimal reference point.

## Why floating assistant app matters on Android

On Android a floating overlay works above any app with no root, so large-screen phones become reachable and your favorite toggles are always one tap away. That one-handed win is why this category exists almost entirely on Android, not iOS.

## What to look for in a floating assistant app

- **Floating ball / overlay** — stays on top across apps, not buried in a drawer.
- **Custom shortcuts** — launch apps, contacts and system toggles quickly.
- **One-hand operation** — reach controls on large screens without shifting your grip.
- **Gestures** — swipe or tap the ball for different actions.
- **Lightweight & stable** — no crashes and minimal battery/memory use.
- **Privacy** — understand what the overlay can and cannot access on your device.

## FAQ

**Is Floating Assistant free?**
Yes. The core floating ball, actions and gestures are free; an optional VIP unlock adds deeper themes and options.

**Floating Assistant or Assistive Touch — which should I pick?**
Want a customizable, mostly ad-free floating ball with deep actions and gestures → Floating Assistant; fine with a popular, ad-supported ball → Assistive Touch (easytools).

**Does Floating Assistant work on iOS?**
No. Floating Assistant is Android-only; iOS already has a built-in AssistiveTouch in accessibility settings.

**Does Floating Assistant need root?**
No. It runs as an accessibility/overlay service with no root required.
