Both put controls on top of your screen. This **Floating Assistant vs Assistive Touch** comparison helps you choose an **overlay widget** app in 2026.

![Floating Assistant vs Assistive Touch](/assets/images/apps/floating-assistant/screen-compare.webp)

## At a glance

| | Floating Assistant | Assistive Touch |
|---|---|---|
| **Assistive touch** | **Yes** | **Yes** |
| **Floating clock** | **Yes** | Usually no |
| **Floating monitor** | **Yes (CPU/RAM/battery)** | No |
| **One-tap boost** | **Yes** | Varies |
| **Customization** | Ball + modules | Size, color, gestures |
| **Price** | Free core + unlock | Free (ads) |

## Floating Assistant, in detail

A **3-in-1 floating assistant**:

- **Assistive Touch** — virtual home, back, recent, volume; one-tap boost, lock screen, screenshot, flashlight, rotation, auto brightness, quick app launch.
- **Floating clock and countdown** — draggable, 24-hour and seconds, position saved, works over fullscreen apps.
- **Floating monitor** — real-time CPU temp, frequency, usage, RAM, battery with curves and one-tap boost.

## Assistive Touch, in detail

The classic free **floating ball** (e.g., Easy Touch):

- Virtual **home, back, recent, volume** buttons.
- **Lock screen, screenshot, flashlight**, quick toggles.
- Customizable **size, color, and gestures**; Material Design.
- Ideal for big phones and protecting worn hardware buttons.
- No clock or live monitor.

## When Floating Assistant wins

- You want **more than buttons** — a clock and a live monitor.
- You game and want **floating CPU/RAM** readouts.
- You like an all-in-one overlay.

## When Assistive Touch wins

- You only need **navigation buttons**.
- You prefer a minimal, familiar floating ball.
- You want a tiny, always-there control.

## Verdict by user type

- **Gamer / power user** → Floating Assistant (monitor + boost).
- **Big-phone one-hander** → either; Assistive Touch is lighter.
- **Just need back/home** → Assistive Touch.

## Bottom line

Pick a basic **assistive touch** app for buttons. Pick **Floating Assistant** for a **3-in-1 overlay with clock and monitor**.
