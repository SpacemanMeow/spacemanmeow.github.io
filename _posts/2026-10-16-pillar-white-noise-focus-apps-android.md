If sleep won't come, focus keeps slipping, or the dorm next door won't quiet down, a white noise app is the cheapest sleep and focus tool on Android. This 2026 guide breaks down what actually matters in a sound app and how Meow FM stacks up against the biggest names like Calm.

## Why white noise app matters on Android

On Android, background playback is the make-or-break feature: the sound has to keep running with the screen off and not torch your battery, because that's exactly when you're trying to sleep. A home-screen clock widget also turns the phone into a bedside display, which is something iPhone widgets do far less naturally.

## What to look for in a white noise app

- **Privacy & account** — most sound apps don't need an account; you just press play. Prefer ones that work fully offline so nothing depends on a server you don't control.
- **Background playback & battery** — audio must continue with the screen locked and sip battery, not drain it overnight while you sleep.
- **Sleep timer** — auto turn-off so the noise stops after you doze off and doesn't run till morning.
- **Sound variety & mixing** — rain, storm, forest, fireplace; ideally layers you can stack (rain + thunder) into a custom scene.
- **Offline playback** — no signal, no problem on a plane, subway or campsite.
- **Clock / focus mode** — a visible clock or focus timer helps study and work sessions, not just sleep.
