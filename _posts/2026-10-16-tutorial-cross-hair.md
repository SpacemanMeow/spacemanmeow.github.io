## App Introduction

Crosshair (Crosshair Aim) adds a **custom crosshair overlay** to FPS games on Android, with **80+ designs** to improve your aim.

**🎯 80+ Styles**
- **84 Basic** (flat) styles built in
- **63 Pro** (advanced) styles
- Free users keep ~30 styles permanently; the rest unlock via a 24-hour reward ad or the Pro app

**🛠 Customize**
- **Scale** 50%–300%
- **Alpha** 30%–100%
- **Color**: 60 colors + a picker
- **Position**: drag with arrow keys (no thickness slider — thickness is baked into each style)

**🔓 No Root, No Accessibility**
- Only the **"Draw over other apps"** (overlay) permission is required

> Note: there are **no per-game presets** — the app keeps a single global crosshair configuration.

## First-time Use

Open the app to the main console.

- A **START/STOP** button shows/hides the crosshair
- Tap the **style** area to pick a design, **color** for color, and use the **scale/alpha** sliders
- The settings gear opens language, rate, share and upgrade

## Grant Overlay Permission

1. Tap the crosshair switch / START
2. If prompted, grant **"display pop-up window"** (Draw over other apps)
3. The app opens system Settings → "Draw over other apps"; enable it for Crosshair
4. Return to the app

This permission (not Accessibility, not root) is what lets the crosshair sit above the game.

## Choose a Crosshair Style

1. Tap the **style** area → **Style** screen
2. Switch **Basic** (5-column grid) / **Advanced** (4-column grid) tabs
3. Tap a thumbnail to apply it
4. Locked Pro styles prompt a reward ad or upgrade

<!-- SCREENSHOT: Style screen: Basic grid (84) and Advanced grid (63) → /assets/images/blog/tutorial_cross_style.webp -->

## Customize Size, Color, Position

- **Scale**: drag the scale slider (50%–300%)
- **Alpha**: drag the alpha slider (30%–100%)
- **Color**: open **Color** → pick from 60 colors or use the picker
- **Position**: use the arrow buttons to nudge; set the step (1–50, default 10) via the step input; or drag the on-screen control panel in-game

There is **no independent thickness slider** — each pre-rendered style already has its own thickness.

## Use It in a Game

1. Enable the crosshair (START)
2. Launch your FPS game
3. The crosshair floats above the game and does **not** intercept touches (so your taps still hit the game)
4. Tap the crosshair to open the floating control panel: size +/−, alpha +/−, arrows, reset, close

## Unlock More Styles

- **Free**: ~30 styles permanent + watch a reward ad to unlock a locked style for **24 hours**
- **Pro** (separate app): no ads and all 147 styles permanent

## FAQ

### Do I need root or Accessibility?
No. Only the overlay (Draw over other apps) permission.

### Can I save a different crosshair per game?
No — the app stores one global configuration. You change it manually when switching games.

### Can I change thickness?
Not separately. Thickness is part of each style's design.

## Summary

Crosshair is the lightest way to add a precise, customizable reticle to any Android shooter — no root, no Accessibility, with 80+ styles and a free tier to start.
