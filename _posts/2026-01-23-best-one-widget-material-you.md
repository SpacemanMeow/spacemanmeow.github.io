Since Android 12, **Material You** lets the system pull a color palette from your wallpaper and spread it across the UI. A **Material You widget** should feel native — rounded, color-matched, and theme-aware. **One Widget** ships cute, theme-friendly widgets that follow your system look. Below we compare it with **KWGT** for Material You styling.

## What Material You widgets need

- **Dynamic color** — hues drawn from the wallpaper.
- **Light/dark follow** — switches with the system theme.
- **Rounded, shape-aware corners** — matches the system grid.
- **Quick placement** — no editor required.

## The contenders at a glance

| App | Price (2026) | Dynamic color | Setup | Theme-aware | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## One Widget, in depth

One Widget is a **cat-themed widget pack** for Android with styles that follow the system theme, including Material You color and corner shaping.

**Strengths for Material You**
- **Theme-matched palettes** — widgets pick up wallpaper-derived colors.
- **Rounded, shape-aware** — corners follow the system grid.
- **Ready-made** — place a clock or weather widget and it already fits.
- **Cute cat styling** — friendly without breaking the material look.
- **Light and local** — no account, no cloud.

**Weaknesses for Material You**
- **Less granular than KWGT** — you adopt the theme, you don't author it.
- **VIP for more** — extra widgets and deeper styling behind the upgrade.

**Best for:** people who want a **Material You widget** that looks native in seconds.

- [Google Play — One Widget](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.glgjing.galaxy.nothing.glass.material.widget)


## KWGT, in depth

KWGT (Kustom Widget Maker, package `org.kustom.widget`) can reproduce Material You exactly — if you write the Kode to read system colors and shape them.

**Strengths for Material You**
- **Pixel-level control** — dynamic color via formulas, custom shapes, animations.
- **One-time Pro Key** — no subscription.

**Weaknesses for Material You**
- **Manual work** — you must build the dynamic-color logic yourself.
- **Steep curve** — not a "place and match" experience.
- **Launcher-dependent** — needs a compatible launcher.

**Best for:** Android themers who want exact control.

- [Google Play — KWGT](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=org.kustom.widget)

## Which should you pick?

- Want **Material You that just matches** → **One Widget**.
- Want **hand-authored dynamic color** → **KWGT**.
