## Start in 3 steps

1. Tap a template like "Drink water".
2. Set a simple target (3 times a day).
3. Check in daily and watch the streak grow.

## Tips that actually stick

- **Start with one habit.** Two at most. "Drink water" beats an ambitious 10-habit plan you'll abandon by week two.
- **Use a template.** Meow Habits' library covers health, study, productivity, and more — begin with one tap.
- **Set a tiny target.** Three times a day is easier to win than "never miss."
- **Review weekly**, not hourly. Streaks reward consistency, not perfection.
