Below we compare the five apps people actually install — **Meow Money Manager, YNAB, Monarch Money, PocketGuard, and EveryDollar** — across price, privacy, currencies, and everyday usability, then show how to choose.

## What a good budget app actually does

A **budget app** turns "where did my money go?" into a number you can act on. The features that matter most:

- **Fast entry** — log an expense in under 10 seconds, ideally offline.
- **Flexible budgets** — total, per-category, and recurring (rent, salary, subscriptions).
- **Multi-currency** — essential if you travel, freelance, or share finances across borders.
- **Privacy** — local storage + lock vs. a cloud account that syncs your bank.
- **Export** — CSV for taxes, reimbursement, or simply leaving the app later.
- **Charts** — pie, bar, and calendar views to spot trends.

## Meow Money Manager in depth

Meow Money Manager is a **cat-themed bookkeeping app** for Android and iOS that stores everything **on your device** behind a fingerprint lock — no sign-up, no bank login, no data leaving your phone unless you back up to iCloud or WebDav.

**Highlights**
- **No account required** — open the app and start logging.
- **Multi-currency ledgers** — 50+ currency symbols, multiple ledgers for home, travel, and business.
- **Budgets** — daily, weekly, monthly, yearly, and per-category, with over-budget alerts.
- **Recurring transactions** — auto-generate rent, salaries, and subscriptions.
- **Reimbursement tracking** — mark expenses reimbursable (full or partial).
- **Asset management** — cash, cards, funds, and net worth.
- **CSV export** plus iCloud / WebDav backup.
- **400+ cute icons** and dark mode.

![Meow Money Manager budget screen](/assets/images/apps/meow-money-manager/screen-budget.webp)

**Where it wins**
- Privacy-first users who refuse cloud accounts.
- Multi-currency households and travelers.
- Anyone who wants a **free budget app** without a paywall on core tracking.

**Where it's lighter**
- No automatic bank sync — you enter transactions manually (a plus for privacy, a minus for hands-off users).
- Heavy automation and coaching reports are shallower than YNAB's.

## How the others compare

- **YNAB** teaches you to "give every dollar a job" with bank sync and strong reports, but it's a paid subscription with a learning curve.
- **Monarch Money** is the closest Mint replacement with a polished net-worth dashboard, yet it's web-first and ~$100/year.
- **PocketGuard** answers one question — "how much is safe to spend?" — but full features need Plus.
- **EveryDollar** mirrors Ramsey's zero-based method; the free tier is manual entry only.

## How to start with Meow Money Manager in 5 steps

1. Create a ledger ("Daily" or "Travel").
2. Tap **+** and log an expense — type, account, amount, note.
3. Set a **monthly budget** and a per-category limit.
4. Turn on **recurring transactions** for rent or subscriptions.
5. Export to **CSV** anytime for reconciliation.
