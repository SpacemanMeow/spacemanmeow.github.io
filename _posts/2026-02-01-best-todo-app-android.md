A reliable **to-do list app** is the difference between a productive day and a forgotten one. In 2026 the Android market is crowded, so we compared the apps people actually install — **Meow Todo, Todoist, TickTick, Microsoft To Do, and Google Tasks** — across price, subtasks, calendar, sync, and privacy, then show how to choose the one you'll keep using.

![Meow Todo list view](/assets/images/apps/meow-todo-list/screen-list.webp)

## What the best task manager should do

The right **task manager** or **schedule planner** should let you:
- **Capture tasks in seconds** — inbox-style quick add.
- **Break work into subtasks** — multi-step assignments stay visible.
- **See deadlines on a calendar** — day, week, and month views.
- **Get reminders** so nothing slips, including daily digests.
- **Sync across phone and tablet** without losing data.

## Meow Todo in depth

Meow Todo is a **cute task manager** for Android and iOS that keeps planning simple and private. Drawn from the app itself:

- **Lists and tags** to organize work, study, and home.
- **Subtasks** for deeper breakdown of any task.
- **Priority** levels (high, medium, low, normal).
- **Recurring tasks** — daily, weekly, monthly, yearly, even end-of-month.
- **Calendar view** (day, week, month) and completion stats.
- **Daily and due-task notifications**.
- **CSV export** plus **Google Drive / WebDav** sync.
- **400+ cute icons** and dark mode.
- **Fingerprint lock** for privacy.

![Meow Todo calendar view](/assets/images/apps/meow-todo-list/screen-calendar.webp)

**Where it wins**
- Free core with no paywall on lists, reminders, or calendar.
- Local-first privacy with your own Drive/WebDav backup.
- CSV export — rare among free to-do apps.

**Where it's lighter**
- No natural-language date parsing ("every mon 9am") — you pick dates manually.
- No team/shared-project collaboration.

## How the others compare

- **Todoist** is the powerhouse: natural-language input, integrations, and karma gamification, but the free tier is limited and Pro is a subscription.
- **TickTick** bundles calendar, pomodoro, and habit tracking; Premium is cheaper than Todoist Pro.
- **Microsoft To Do** is free and tightly integrated with Outlook — ideal if you live in 365.
- **Google Tasks** is minimal but perfect if your life runs on Gmail and Calendar.

## How to start with Meow Todo in 4 steps

1. Create a list (for example "Study" or "Home").
2. Add a task, set priority and due date.
3. Add **subtasks** and turn on recurring if needed.
4. Open the **calendar** and review what is due this week.

## Which should you pick?

- Want **free, private, with CSV export** → **Meow Todo**.
- Want **natural-language + integrations** → Todoist.
- Want **calendar + pomodoro in one** → TickTick.
- Already on **Microsoft 365** → Microsoft To Do.
- Live in **Gmail** → Google Tasks.
