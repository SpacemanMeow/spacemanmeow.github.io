_Last updated: September 16, 2026_

Meow Apps Studio ("we", "us") builds mobile applications for iOS and Android. This Privacy Policy
explains what information our apps and this website collect, how it is used, and the choices you have.

## Information we collect

**Data you create in our apps.** Content you enter (for example records, tasks, habits or diary
entries) is stored locally on your device by default. We do not upload it to our servers, and no
account is required to use the apps.

**Purchase information.** If you buy a subscription or a one-time purchase, the transaction is
processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We receive only the receipt or subscription
status needed to unlock paid features — never your full payment details.

**Diagnostics.** Apps may send anonymous crash reports and basic usage statistics to help us fix
problems. These reports contain no personal content and cannot be used to identify you.

**This website.** Our website is static and does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Standard
server logs may be kept by our hosting provider for security and reliability purposes.

## How we use information

- To provide and maintain the features of our apps.
- To validate purchases and unlock paid functionality.
- To diagnose crashes and improve stability.
- To respond to support requests you send us.

We do **not** sell your personal information, and we do not use your content for advertising.

## Third-party services

Our apps and website may rely on the following providers, each governed by their own privacy policy:

- Apple App Store
- Google Play
- GitHub Pages (website hosting)

## Children's privacy

Our apps are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information
from them.

## Your rights

Because your data is stored on your device, you can view, export or delete it at any time from within
the app or by removing the app. If you have sent us a support request and would like it deleted,
contact us using the address below.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the
"Last updated" date above.
