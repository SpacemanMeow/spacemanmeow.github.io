One Widget is your ultimate all-in-one Android home screen customization toolkit, packed with 500+ premium aesthetic widgets covering four trending design styles: Glass iOS style, Material You, Galaxy One UI (Samsung), and Nothing minimalist style. Perfect for users who love personalized, clean, and functional home screens, this app combines beauty and practicality to upgrade your Android desktop experience in one tap.

**Four Exclusive Popular Widget Styles**

No need to install multiple widget apps! We integrate the most mainstream popular design styles in 2026. The Glass style restores transparent frosted iOS visual effects for a sleek blurred aesthetic. The latest Material You dynamic style adapts to system wallpaper colors, fitting pure Android native aesthetics. The highly restored Galaxy One UI style is specially optimized for Samsung Galaxy devices with official UI details. The ultra-clean Nothing minimalist style brings low-saturation, futuristic desktop visuals for pure and tidy home screens.

**8 Major Functional Widget Categories & Full Customization**

1. App Shortcut Widgets: Rich multi-size layouts including 1x1, 1x2, 2x2, 1x4, 1x5, 1x6, 2x4, 2x5, 2x6. Freely customize icon background color and corner radius to match your desktop theme perfectly.
2. System Quick Toggle Widgets: One-click control of core system functions, including WiFi, Mobile Data, Bluetooth, Torch, Screen Rotation, Airplane Mode, Hotspot, Do Not Disturb, Location Service, Vibration Mode, and Dark Mode, greatly improving daily operation efficiency.
3. Device Hardware Info Widgets: Real-time display of phone battery level, detailed battery status, RAM memory usage, and internal storage data, helping you monitor device status at a glance.
4. Clock & Calendar Widgets: Various stylish digital clock widgets with diverse formats, paired with practical calendar components to keep track of time and schedule easily.
5. Notes & Quotes Widgets: Built-in memo checklist and inspirational quote widgets, perfect for daily to-do reminders and positive mood adjustment.
6. Quick Contact Widgets: Add frequently used contacts to desktop for one-click call and contact access, saving time.
7. Aggregated App Widgets: Exclusive integrated tool sets. Google Suite covers Chrome, Drive, Maps, Photos, Play, YouTube, Mail, Calendar. AI Toolkit includes Claude, Copilot, Gemini, GPT, Grok, Perplexity. Social Media Suite supports Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, Snapchat, Telegram, TikTok, WhatsApp, X, meeting all quick launch needs.
8. Practical Mini Tools Widgets: Built-in Compass, Pedometer, Counter, and Calculator widgets, integrating daily essential tools without extra app installation.

**Core Advantages**

One Widget integrates 500+ high-quality premium widgets. We keep updating and adding new widgets continuously to bring you more fresh desktop customization options, with multi-style adaptation and full free customization. It is a must-have personalization tool for Android users, suitable for Samsung Galaxy, Pixel, Nothing and all Android devices. Transform your ordinary home screen into a stylish, efficient and smart desktop with just one app!
