### Core Features of Meow Habits

**500+ Cute Cartoon Icons - Fit All Habit Scenarios**

Both aesthetically pleasing and practical—this habit tracker is a feast for the eyes! Our lovely cartoon icon library covers all scenarios, from fitness and health to work, study, and housework. With 500+ personalized icons, it perfectly matches every habit you want to track, adding a touch of ritual to your habit journey and keeping you motivated to persist.

**Multi Habit Tracking & Statistics - Drive Efficient Habit Building**

Create multiple habits at once, with support for daily/weekly/monthly/custom cycles. Recording progress is a breeze with one tap—no complicated setup, just hassle-free habit tracking! Visual charts like bar graphs, pie charts, and calendar views let you keep tabs on your habit streaks, completion rates, and long-term trends. Let data deliver intuitive feedback on your efforts, helping you adjust strategies in time, avoid blind persistence, and truly streamline your habit-building workflow.

**Fingerprint Lock + Offline Privacy Protection - Track Habits Without Interruption**

We go the extra mile to safeguard your exclusive habit data—set up fingerprint unlock for fully private and controllable records! No internet? No problem. Track habits and update progress anytime, anywhere offline—never miss a beat whether you’re at home, work, or on the go. All records are securely stored, putting you in complete control of your data so you can focus on habit building without worries.

**Report Export - Analyze Habit Trends Precisely**

Export CSV reports to back up your habit data effortlessly, saving you time and effort on offline analysis. Dig into your habit patterns, optimize your execution plan, and make your habit building more targeted for even better results.

**Multi Backup & Restore - Data Security Guaranteed**

Compatible with Google Drive and WebDav, this habit tracker offers automated backups when you add records, plus manual backup options—double protection against data loss. Enjoy a seamless experience even when switching devices: restore all your habit tracking records in a flash and keep your consistency journey on track.

**Dark/Light Mode - Comfortable Habit Tracking**

Freely switch between two carefully calibrated themes, both easy on the eyes with a healing, soft visual feel. Adapt perfectly to daytime work and nighttime recording, making habit tracking comfortable and keeping your execution efficiency high.
