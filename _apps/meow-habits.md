Meow Habits is a habit tracker that makes consistency feel easy. Create a habit, check in every
day, and watch your streak grow — all wrapped in a soft, cat-themed interface.

**Features of Meow Habits - complete guide:**

### Habit tracker

Track positive habits and break bad ones with daily check-ins.

### Multiple views

Heat maps, streaks and calendars help you understand your progress.

### Simple operation

One tap is all it takes to record today's habit.

### Gentle reminders

Customizable reminders that nudge you without nagging.
