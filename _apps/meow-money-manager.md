Meow Money Manager bookkeeping is a financial app for your personal finances management. This
finance app will help you take your budget, money and finances under control. The expense and
income tracker make your budget wallet and every credit card very clearly. You won't need to dig
through your wallet or check your bank account to be aware of your financial circumstances. With
this budget expense and income tracker finances app you can easily track your money.

**Features of Meow Money Manager - complete guide:**

### Cute record category icons

400+ cute icons for you to custom your own money manager. Meow Money Manager has various record
types, you can choose the icon for your consumption habit.

### Budget tracker & category budget

The budget for daily, monthly or annual period. You can also set up category budget for any expense
or income category, the total budget and all the category budgets will be tracked clearly at the
same time.

### Instant and powerful chart statistics

Reports of financial recording activities daily, monthly, and yearly. Make it easier for you to
track your finances.

### Reimbursement management

When your expenses need to be reimbursed, like a business trip, you just need check the reimburse
button on the add record page, then the Meow Manager will track the reimbursement automatically.
You can check and modify the reimbursement status at any time.

### Support multiple ledgers

You can add different ledger for tracking different expense or income, and you check the records
for every ledger individually. Select the ledger or all the ledger at same time.
