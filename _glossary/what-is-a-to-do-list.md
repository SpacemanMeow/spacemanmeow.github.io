A **to-do list** (or task list) is a written set of tasks you intend to complete, often with due dates, priorities, and checkboxes. Task management is the broader practice of capturing, organizing, and finishing work.

## Why lists boost productivity

- **Offload memory:** writing tasks down frees your mind to focus.
- **Prioritization:** ordering items shows what matters today versus later.
- **Closure:** checking a box gives a small dopamine reward that sustains momentum.

## How to-do apps help

An app like Meow To-Do List turns a paper list into a flexible system: recurring tasks, reminders, sub-tasks, and lists per project. The key habit is capture—get everything out of your head into one trusted place—then review daily. A good to-do list is less about doing more and more about doing the right things.
