## Definition

**A subtask** is a smaller, independently checkable unit of work that lives inside a parent task. Instead of one vague "Plan trip" item, you break it into "Book flight", "Reserve hotel", and "Make itinerary" — each with its own done state.

## Key ideas

- **Decompose big tasks:** split a project into actionable steps.
- **Track progress:** see what's finished versus remaining at a glance.
- **Keep context:** subtasks stay attached to their parent, not scattered.

## Why it matters in a to-do app

Subtasks turn intention into a checklist you can tick off, which boosts completion and reduces overwhelm. Meow Todo List lets you add subtasks under any task, check them off individually, and view an overall progress bar for the parent task.
