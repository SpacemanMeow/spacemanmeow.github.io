A **flip clock** is a clock display that shows the time using split-flap cards -- the classic mechanical panels that flip down to reveal the next digit, like old train-station departure boards.

## Digital flip clocks

On phones, a flip clock app (such as Only Clock) recreates that look with an animated flip animation for hours and minutes, often as a fullscreen nightstand or focus clock.

## Why people use them

- The flipping motion makes the passage of each minute feel tangible and calming.
- A fullscreen flip clock doubles as a desk or bedside clock.
- Minimalist flip designs fit focus sessions and pomodoro timers.

Flip clocks are valued for aesthetics and focus, not for reading the time at a glance like a digital readout.
