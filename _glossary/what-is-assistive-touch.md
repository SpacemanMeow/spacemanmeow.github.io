## Definition & What It Does

**Assistive Touch** is an accessibility feature—popularized on iOS—that adds a floating on-screen button you can tap to reach common actions (home, screenshot, volume, shortcuts) without using physical hardware keys.

## Why people use it

- **Hardware relief:** reduces wear on physical buttons.
- **One-handed use:** big phones are hard to reach; a movable button helps.
- **Custom shortcuts:** map taps to frequently used actions.

## Relation to floating assistant apps

On Android, a **floating assistant** app like Floating Assistant provides a similar always-on bubble. It floats above other apps, so you can trigger actions, open a shortcut, or launch a tool without leaving your current screen. Assistive Touch is the concept; a floating assistant is one Android implementation of that idea—bringing quick controls to where your finger already is.
