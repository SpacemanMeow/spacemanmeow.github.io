A **mood tracker** is any tool—paper journal, spreadsheet, or app like Meow Mood Diary—that records how you feel on a given day, often with a rating (such as 1–5), tags (stressed, calm, energetic), and optional notes.

## Why people track mood

- **Self-awareness:** spotting patterns (for example, low mood every Sunday) is the first step to change.
- **Conversation aid:** a history helps you and a therapist or doctor see trends over weeks, not just today.
- **Habit linkage:** many apps overlay mood against sleep, exercise, or period data to reveal possible causes.

## How mood tracker apps work

You log a mood in seconds; the app charts it over time and may send a daily reminder. Some infer mood from journal text. The goal is not diagnosis—it is a private, low-effort log that makes emotional patterns visible so you can act on them.
