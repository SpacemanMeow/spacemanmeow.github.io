## Definition

**Budget management** is the continuous practice of planning how much money you can spend, tracking actual spending against that plan, and adjusting when you go off course. It turns a vague "spend less" goal into concrete category limits — food, transport, entertainment — that you can actually measure.

## Key ideas

- **Plan first:** set a spending cap for each category before the month begins.
- **Track continuously:** record expenses as they happen so the budget stays current.
- **React to overspend:** get alerted or review reports when a category nears its limit.

## Why it matters in a finance app

A budgeting app removes the spreadsheet busywork: it totals each category automatically, shows the remaining balance at a glance, and flags overspending. Meow Money Manager lets you set per-category budgets, monitors totals in real time, and surfaces reports so you can tighten or relax a limit before the month ends.
