A **habit tracker** is a tool—often a mobile app like Meow Habits—for recording whether you performed a chosen behavior each day (meditate, read, drink water) and showing your progress over time.

## Why habit tracking works

- **Visibility:** a simple daily check-in turns a vague intention into a tracked action.
- **Consistency over intensity:** doing a little every day beats occasional bursts; trackers reward the streak.
- **Feedback loop:** seeing a filled calendar reinforces the behavior and surfaces skipped days early.

## How habit tracker apps work

You define habits and check in once a day. The app stores each completion and renders calendars, streaks, and statistics. Many let you group habits, set reminders, and skip without breaking the chain. Combined with a streak counter, a habit tracker turns repetition into automaticity—the foundation of lasting change.
