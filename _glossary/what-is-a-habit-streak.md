A **habit streak** is the count of consecutive days you complete a chosen habit without a miss. Habit tracker apps record each check-in and display the running streak so you can see how many days in a row you've kept the habit.

## Why streaks matter

- A visible streak turns an abstract goal into a daily, game-like commitment.
- Missing a day resets the counter to zero, which nudges you to stay consistent.
- Long streaks are strongly correlated with habit formation because repetition builds automaticity.

## How streak counters work

Most trackers (including Meow Habits) compare today's check-in against the previous day. If you check in on consecutive calendar days, the streak increments; if you skip a day, it resets. Some apps offer a "grace day" or weekly target so a single slip doesn't wipe progress.

A streak is a motivator, not a punishment — the goal is consistent practice, not a perfect record.
