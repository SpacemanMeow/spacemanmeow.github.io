## Definition & Why Use One

A **nightstand clock** is a bedside display—traditionally a physical alarm clock—that shows the time, date, and alarms at a glance while you are in bed. On a phone, a nightstand mode turns the device into that display.

## Why phone users run one

- **Glanceable time:** large, dim clock you can read without unlocking.
- **Alarm hub:** set and manage alarms in one place.
- **Ambiance:** a soft, dark-friendly display suits a bedroom better than a bright app.

## How flip-clock styles fit

Apps like Only Clock offer a **flip-clock** look—large split digits that mimic a mechanical flap clock—ideal for a nightstand: high contrast, minimal, and easy to read half-asleep. A nightstand clock is essentially a focused, low-stimulus time display designed for the bedroom, not a full-featured app.
