## Definition

**Reimbursement management** is the workflow of recording expenses you paid on someone else's behalf, submitting them for repayment, and tracking whether the money has come back. It matters for both employees claiming work costs and individuals who share bills with friends or family.

## Key ideas

- **Mark reimbursable:** tag an expense as "to be reimbursed" at entry time.
- **Track status:** know which claims are pending, approved, or repaid.
- **Reconcile later:** match the repayment against the original expense.

## Why it matters in a finance app

Without a system, reimbursable spending blends into normal outflows and you forget what's owed. Meow Money Manager lets you flag reimbursable entries, keep them separate from personal spending, and review outstanding amounts so nothing slips through the cracks.
