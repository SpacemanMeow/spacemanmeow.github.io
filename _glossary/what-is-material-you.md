**Material You** is Google's design language, introduced with Android 12, built around personalization and adaptive color. The headline feature is dynamic color: the system extracts a palette from your wallpaper and applies it across the UI and supported apps.

## Key ideas

- **Dynamic color:** buttons, backgrounds, and widgets recolor to match your wallpaper.
- **Personal:** the device feels owned, not generic.
- **Consistency:** one theme flows through system and apps.

## Why widgets adopt it

When a widget supports Material You, its colors shift with your theme, so it blends into the home screen instead of clashing. Tools like One Widget embrace this, offering widgets that automatically restyle as you change wallpapers. Material You is less a single look and more a system for making the whole interface feel coherent and uniquely yours.
