A **home screen widget** is a live, interactive mini-view of an app that sits directly on your Android launcher—no need to open the full app. Examples: a clock, a to-do checklist, a weather tile, a music player.

## Why widgets matter

- **At-a-glance info:** see time, tasks, or stats without tapping in.
- **Quick actions:** check a box, skip a track, or start a timer from the home screen.
- **Personalization:** arrange your day as a custom dashboard.

## How they work on Android

Widgets are built with the AppWidget framework; size and content are defined by the app. A tool like One Widget emphasizes clean, customizable widgets—including flip-clock and Material You styles—that match your wallpaper and theme. Well-designed widgets turn the home screen from a list of icons into a functional command center.
