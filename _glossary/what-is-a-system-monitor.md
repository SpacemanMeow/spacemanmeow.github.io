## Definition & What It Shows

A **system monitor** is an app—like System Monitor—that displays your device's live internal state: CPU usage, memory (RAM), battery health, storage, and network activity, usually with real-time charts.

## What a system monitor shows

- **CPU:** per-core load, temperature, and clock speed.
- **Memory:** used vs. free RAM and which apps consume the most.
- **Battery:** level, temperature, and discharge rate.
- **Network & storage:** data usage and space breakdown.

## Why Android users run one

Unlike desktops, phones hide these internals. A system monitor makes them visible so you can find the app draining battery, confirm whether more RAM helps, or check temperatures during gaming. It is a diagnostic dashboard—read-only insight that helps you decide when to close apps, free memory, or cool the device down.
